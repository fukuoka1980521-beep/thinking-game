## Research-boundary statement

This file records publication metadata only. It does **not** modify the frozen confirmatory dataset, episode counts, trigger definitions, shadow results, or interpretation boundary.

The confirmatory field dataset remains frozen at:
- 6 natural mandatory-trigger episodes;
- 4 independent trigger-positive task families;
- at least 2 trigger-negative ordinary task families;
- 6/6 leakage-free shadow pairs;
- 0/6 post-detection mutations before required reassessment.

Any stronger causal study or later field episode must be versioned as a separate prospective research phase.
