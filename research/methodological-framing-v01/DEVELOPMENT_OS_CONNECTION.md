## Do not include it in the first confirmatory Study A

Reason:
- the framework is user-developed;
- the study motivation arose partly from observing its effects;
- including it during initial hypothesis formation creates avoidable researcher-interest and design-bias concerns.

The first confirmatory experiment should use only externally established methodological families.

## Recommended sequence

1. Establish whether generic methodological framing changes research-plan structure.
2. Establish fixed-evidence and evidence-selection effects.
3. Freeze all scoring and analysis rules.
4. Only then introduce Development OS as a previously unseen held-out treatment.
5. Compare its structural signature against both CONTROL and standard method families.
