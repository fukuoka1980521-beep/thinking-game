# NEW LIFE — How to Play (Owner Playtest Copy)

This is a 30-day text game, run for you by an AI acting as Game Master. Each day has one main
scene; you'll usually get a short list of choices, and sometimes a chance to talk with someone
(free talk) in your own words. Play it day by day, in order -- like any gamebook, it's more
enjoyable played straight through than skimmed ahead.

There's no "correct" choice. Some choices open a conversation; some just let you observe or move on.
Declining to get involved is always a legitimate way to play -- the town keeps going either way.
The Game Master will never tell you which choice matters or hint at what's coming -- if you ask for
a hint, expect a gentle, in-character non-answer rather than a real one. That's by design, not a
bug.

## Who you'll meet

Six people live around the same street. This is only what you'd notice on Day 1 -- everything else
is worth discovering as you play, not reading ahead about.

- **Hina** -- getting a small, half-finished shop ready to open, upstairs from where you're staying.
- **Yohei** -- runs the general store down the street.
- **Daisuke** -- fixes chairs and furniture out of a small shop; an old friend of Yohei's.
- **Jin** -- the town's handyman; you'll often notice his work after the fact, not while it's
  happening.
- **Miyoko** -- runs the café; knows everyone's business, in the ordinary small-town way.
- **Fumiko** -- organizes the town Hall and, this month, the upcoming street festival.

## What the month is building toward

A street festival happens partway through the month. The four weeks around it are made of ordinary
days -- small conversations, small chores, small decisions -- more than dramatic ones. Paying
attention to people is never required to finish the game, but it does change what you get to see.

## Playing it

Give a fresh AI chat both files in this package (this one, and `NEWLIFE_30DAY_OWNER_GAME_MASTER_
V1.md`), and ask it to run the game for you as Game Master, starting from Day 1, one day at a time,
waiting for your response before advancing. Then just play -- answer in your own words when a
conversation is open, or pick from the choices given. That's the whole interface.
