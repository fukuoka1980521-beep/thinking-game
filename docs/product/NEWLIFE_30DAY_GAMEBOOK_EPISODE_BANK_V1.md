# NEW LIFE 30-Day Gamebook — Episode Bank V1 (Phase A)

50 fictional, anonymized episode seeds. None of these are transcriptions of real tester/user data
from this repo's Case1 material or any other real consultation record -- they are original small-
town situations built from ordinary human patterns (money, pride, aging, generational gaps,
embarrassment, help/refusal-of-help, small successes, comedy, quiet days), the same domain
knowledge this project's whole "thinking game" concept already draws on, never a real person's
story. Each is a *seed*, not a finished scene -- the story spine (Phase C) picks and sequences a
subset of these; the rest remain a bank for future days/replays, per the instruction not to force
every seed into the 30 days.

Field key: RAW HUMAN CORE (the universal pattern) / INITIAL INTERPRETATION (what it looks like at
first) / HIDDEN INFO (what's true underneath, revealed later or never) / CONFLICT / PLAYER
INTERVENTION POINT / POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE / SUITABLE NPC / CALLBACK POTENTIAL.

## Hina (26, bakery owner-to-be)

**E01 — The rent envelope**
RAW HUMAN CORE: money is tighter than the confident front suggests. INITIAL INTERPRETATION: Hina
mentions "juggling a few things" this week, sounds casual. HIDDEN INFO: this month's rent is a real
stretch; last month's shop closure was about money, not "moving on." CONFLICT: her pride vs. her
actual situation. PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: player can either let the comment pass or gently ask
"juggling how?" POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: asking opens a small trust step; letting it pass is equally
valid, just slower. SUITABLE NPC: Hina. CALLBACK POTENTIAL: high -- feeds directly into E03/E36-tier
opening-day thread.

**E02 — The burnt batch**
RAW HUMAN CORE: a private small failure, hidden out of embarrassment. INITIAL INTERPRETATION: Hina
seems distracted, shelf half-restocked. HIDDEN INFO: she burned an entire test batch that morning
and threw it out before anyone saw. CONFLICT: wanting to seem competent vs. the truth of a bad
morning. PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: noticing the smell/an odd detail and asking, vs. not noticing.
POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: a small, warm moment if noticed; nothing lost if not. SUITABLE NPC: Hina.
CALLBACK POTENTIAL: medium -- she can reference it self-deprecatingly weeks later once the shop is
going well.

**E03 — Why the last shop really closed**
RAW HUMAN CORE: a private history reframed to sound better than it was. INITIAL INTERPRETATION:
Hina says she "moved on" from her last shop. HIDDEN INFO: it closed because she ran out of money,
not because she chose to leave. CONFLICT: the gap between the story she tells and what happened.
PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: a deep-conversation moment late enough in the relationship for her to
actually say it, never forceable early. POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: real trust deepening, or she simply
never volunteers it -- both fine. SUITABLE NPC: Hina. CALLBACK POTENTIAL: very high -- the single
biggest private-fact reveal available for her arc.

**E04 — Unsolicited advice**
RAW HUMAN CORE: an older person offers advice that lands as condescension. INITIAL INTERPRETATION:
a regular customer tells Hina how she "should" be running things. HIDDEN INFO: the customer means
well and has actually run a shop before. CONFLICT: pride vs. useful information delivered badly.
PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: player can help Hina hear the useful part, or just commiserate.
POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: either is fine; commiserating doesn't "fail" anything. SUITABLE NPC: Hina.
CALLBACK POTENTIAL: low-medium, one-off flavor.

**E05 — First real taste-test**
RAW HUMAN CORE: a small, earned success. INITIAL INTERPRETATION: Hina asks the player to try
something. HIDDEN INFO: none -- this one is exactly what it looks like. CONFLICT: none, deliberately
a low-conflict good-news beat. PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: honest reaction vs. polite reaction (both
valid, no "correct" answer scored). POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: a genuinely warm, uncomplicated moment.
SUITABLE NPC: Hina. CALLBACK POTENTIAL: medium -- the item can reappear on her eventual opening-day
menu.

**E06 — Misreading Yohei**
RAW HUMAN CORE: a newcomer reads an old-timer's gruffness as personal rejection. INITIAL
INTERPRETATION: Hina thinks Yohei doesn't like her. HIDDEN INFO: Yohei is like this with every new
face in town, not just her (Section 13's established canon: he's "still half-skeptical of the new
person" as a pattern, not personal). CONFLICT: her insecurity vs. the actual, more boring truth.
PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: player can pass this observation along to either of them or stay out of
it. POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: a small cross-NPC thaw if the player connects them; nothing broken if not.
SUITABLE NPC: Hina (told to player), resolved via Yohei. CALLBACK POTENTIAL: high -- direct feed
into the Yohei/Hina relationship thread.

**E07 — Two new-ish business owners**
RAW HUMAN CORE: shared struggle creates fast camaraderie. INITIAL INTERPRETATION: Hina and Daisuke
compare notes on paperwork/permits. HIDDEN INFO: none. CONFLICT: none -- a quiet, good day.
PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: optional; the scene can happen with or without the player present.
POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: establishes an NPC-NPC tie independent of the player (per Phase B's explicit
requirement). SUITABLE NPC: Hina + Daisuke. CALLBACK POTENTIAL: medium.

**E08 — The gossip**
RAW HUMAN CORE: small towns talk, and it's not always kind. INITIAL INTERPRETATION: Hina overhears
someone wondering aloud "how long she'll last." HIDDEN INFO: it's idle chatter, not a real town
consensus. CONFLICT: whether to let it get to her. PLAYER INTERVENTION POINT: player can reassure
her or just listen. POSSIBLE CONSEQUENCE: no mechanical effect required -- purely emotional beat.
SUITABLE NPC: Hina. CALLBACK POTENTIAL: low, one-off.
