## Full per-NPC design (Section 11 fields, MAIN SIX only)

### 陽菜 Hina — 26, bakery owner-to-be

- **Location**: 商店街 (her shop-in-progress)
- **Visual identity / silhouette**: youngest and shortest of the six; hair often tied back/covered
  (kitchen-practical), flour-dusted apron over ordinary clothes -- the only MAIN NPC whose default
  pose involves visible manual work-in-progress (measuring, wiping a counter), not standing still
- **Personality**: warm but visibly not-yet-settled; talks fast about the shop, goes quiet about
  anything else
- **Speech rhythm**: bursts of enthusiasm about baking specifics, clipped and short on anything
  personal
- **Wants**: the shop to open without repeating her last town's failure
- **Worries**: that she'll fail here the same way
- **Hides**: the real reason the last shop closed (money, not "just moving")
- **Relationships**: Miyoko already looks out for her (existing canon: brought her food while she
  was setting up); Yohei is still half-skeptical of the "new person" pattern
- **Day 1 state**: shop not open yet, visibly mid-setup
- **Day 7 possible state**: shop open (if player engaged with her prep at least once or twice) or
  still not open, with a different, quieter reason why (never framed as the player's fault)
- **Only-Hina event**: the shop's actual opening day -- a real, dated, system-owned event, not a
  chat topic
- **Return-tomorrow hook**: "何日か後に焼き菓子の試作ができる" -- a dated future promise the system
  tracks, not "また来てね"

### 大輔 Daisuke — 45, barber

- **Location**: 商店街 (かどや, his shop, physically adjacent/part of the shopping street)
- **Visual identity / silhouette**: solid mid-career build, apron/smock with scissors-and-comb
  visible, mid-length practical haircut of his own -- deliberately the "ordinary competent adult"
  silhouette the roster currently has none of
- **Personality**: naturally a listener (occupational habit, not a superpower); deflects when asked
  about himself
- **Speech rhythm**: slow, comfortable pauses; talks more easily mid-task (cutting hair) than
  face-to-face
- **Wants**: to finally decide on the shop renovation he's been putting off 3 months
- **Worries**: that deciding means admitting he's been stalling
- **Hides**: he's lost touch with a friend from his city years and hasn't answered their last
  New Year's card
- **Relationships**: long relationship with Yohei (comes in for a shave); Fumiko is a regular
  customer and a real confidante
- **Day 1 state**: renovation quote still sitting in a drawer
- **Day 7 possible state**: decided (either way) if the player engaged with this thread at least
  twice; untouched otherwise -- his own arc, not gated on the player
- **Only-Daisuke event**: an actual haircut -- the one MAIN NPC interaction that is a literal,
  distinct physical activity (Section 35), not a conversation with props
- **Return-tomorrow hook**: "見積もりの返事をどうするか、もう少し考えると言っていた"

### 相馬迅 Jin — 57, independent handyman

- **Location**: 集会所 (does odd jobs there and around town; no fixed shop)
- **Visual identity / silhouette**: tallest/broadest build of the six, work clothes with visible
  wear, tool belt or a tool actually in hand in his default pose -- reads as "mid-task" by design
- **Personality**: shows rather than explains; fixes things nobody asked him to fix
- **Speech rhythm**: very short sentences, comfortable silences, terse humor
- **Wants**: enough work to stay busy; secretly enjoys being needed
- **Worries**: never voiced directly -- whether working alone forever is really the plan
- **Hides**: he'd take a steady arrangement if offered right, but would never ask for one
- **Relationships**: 30-year friendship with Yohei (comfortable silence, not chatty); does small
  jobs for Fumiko at the hall
- **Day 1 state**: not yet eligible to be asked for repeat help (existing `minDayForHelp` gate)
- **Day 7 possible state**: a standing arrangement started (existing trajectory-accept path) or
  quietly not, with no judgment attached either way
- **Only-Jin event**: the existing trajectory opportunity ("定期的に手伝う気はあるか") -- this
  phase's Big Choice candidate (Section 28/18), reusing the already-built, already-tested engine
- **Return-tomorrow hook**: "今日手伝ったことの続きがある" / the standing-arrangement follow-up

### 洋平 Yohei — 63, general store owner

- **Location**: 洋平商店
- **Visual identity / silhouette**: stocky, weathered, apron over work clothes, often mid-task
  (stacking, checking a delivery) -- the "settled shopkeeper" silhouette, visually the heaviest/
  most grounded of the six
- **Personality**: kind but deflects anything past small talk; changes the subject fast when it's
  about his son
- **Speech rhythm**: short, practical, warms slightly once you're a familiar face
- **Wants**: the shop to keep running smoothly, especially around the shōtengai festival
- **Worries**: no one to hand the shop to
- **Hides**: hasn't really talked to his son in over a year
- **Relationships**: Jin (old friend), Miyoko (trade produce/beans), Hina (still wary of the
  "new person," existing canon)
- **Day 1 state**: ordinary open-for-business day
- **Day 7 possible state**: has said one real sentence about the succession worry (if the player
  built enough familiarity) or hasn't, and that's a legitimate, non-punishing outcome
- **Only-Yohei event**: the festival stock-prep thread (already partially authored in existing
  content) -- reworked into a small, dated "the shipment situation resolves on day N" beat
- **Return-tomorrow hook**: "祭りの仕入れがどうなったか" (a dated, system-tracked outcome)

### 美代子 Miyoko — 68, café owner

- **Location**: 喫茶のどか
- **Visual identity / silhouette**: soft/rounded build, apron over cardigan, the warmest facial
  expression of the six by design (the "you are always welcome here" anchor) -- distinct from
  Yohei's stockier, more work-postured silhouette even though both are 60s
- **Personality**: warm to everyone equally (not special treatment -- important so the player
  doesn't read early warmth as a reward for good play); occasionally mixes up who-said-what
- **Speech rhythm**: unhurried, conversational, asks small questions back
- **Wants**: the café to still feel like somewhere people want to sit
- **Worries**: her daughter keeps pushing her to close up and move closer
- **Hides**: some days she's lonelier than she lets on
- **Relationships**: Yohei (produce trade), Jin (fixes her chairs), Hina (looks out for her),
  Fumiko (old friend since childhood)
- **Day 1 state**: open, ordinary
- **Day 7 possible state**: the daughter conversation is mentioned once more, differently, if the
  player has been a regular -- never resolved into "she decides to close" or "she decides to stay"
  within 7 days (that's a 30-day-scale thread, correctly deferred)
- **Only-Miyoko event**: the literal ORDER → DRINK → CONVERSATION sequence (Section 34) -- the
  one place in the game where "sit down and have something" is its own complete, satisfying unit
- **Return-tomorrow hook**: "新しい豆を試してみる話をしていた" (a dated, concrete callback)

### 文子 Fumiko — 70, retired teacher, community hall

- **Location**: 集会所
- **Visual identity / silhouette**: upright, brisk posture (ex-teacher carriage), practical
  cardigan, reading glasses on a cord -- the only MAIN NPC whose default pose is "already doing
  something administrative" (tidying a noticeboard), distinct from Miyoko's soft warmth and
  Yohei's stocky groundedness despite being the oldest of the six
- **Personality**: brisk, no-nonsense, secretly soft; asks favors bluntly
- **Speech rhythm**: short declarative sentences, occasional leftover teacher-tone
- **Wants**: the hall to stay a place people actually drop into
- **Worries**: a wobbly bench she hasn't gotten fixed yet; a former student she's lost touch with
- **Hides**: she's lonelier since retiring than her brisk manner suggests
- **Relationships**: old friend of Miyoko's; asks Jin for small repairs; keeps half an eye on Hina
- **Day 1 state**: the noticeboard has something new on it (a real, lightweight discover-hook)
- **Day 7 possible state**: the bench got fixed (if the player connected her to Jin) or didn't --
  either is a fine, legible outcome
- **Only-Fumiko event**: community-hall event prep (the one place "the town has a shared thing
  coming up" lives, per Section 22/29's "day the relationships cross" beat)
- **Return-tomorrow hook**: "集会所の準備がどうなったか"
