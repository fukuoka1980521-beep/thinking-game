# NEW LIFE 30-Day Gamebook — Character Arcs V1 (Phase B)

Uses the existing MAIN SIX exactly as already canonized in `NEWLIFE_CHARACTER_ROSTER_V2.md` and the
NPC-NPC relationship graph in that same document (Section 13) -- no new characters invented, no
existing facts contradicted. Each arc extends that canon with the 8 fields this phase requires.
Shizuko (Fortune House) stays a light, secondary recurring presence exactly as her own doc already
scopes her -- not one of the 6 arcs, per that document's own classification.

Every arc is written to be genuinely optional and non-linear: a player who never engages a given NPC
still gets a coherent, unpunished 30 days: matches this project's own recurring
"never a forced quest, never a punishment for declining" discipline.

## Hina, 26 — bakery owner-to-be

- WANT: the shop to actually open, and to stay open, this time.
- FEAR: repeating her last town's failure -- not "failing" in the abstract, specifically *this*
  failure again.
- BLIND SPOT: she reads Yohei's default gruffness as personal rejection when it's really just how he
  treats every newcomer (E06) -- she doesn't yet see the pattern is about him, not her.
- CONTRADICTION: fast and confident about baking specifics, but goes quiet and vague the instant
  anything personal comes up -- competence and self-doubt sitting side by side.
- RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NPCs: Miyoko already looks out for her (brought her food while setting up);
  Yohei still half-skeptical; a fast, easy camaraderie forms with Daisuke over shared new-business
  paperwork (E07); Fumiko keeps a light watchful eye on her.
- PRIVATE FACT: the last shop closed because of money, not because she "moved on" (E03).
- CHANGE POSSIBILITY: the shop opens by Day 30 (state-driven on real engagement, e.g. tied loosely
  to the festival per E48) -- or it doesn't, and that's an equally legitimate, non-punishing outcome
  with its own quieter, still-dignified reason (per existing Day-7-scale precedent, scaled up).
- THING SHE WILL NOT SAY ON DAY 1: that she's genuinely scared this could go the same way as last
  time.

## Daisuke, 45 — barber

- WANT: to finally decide about the renovation he's been sitting on for three months.
- FEAR: that deciding at all means admitting he's been avoiding it, not just "hasn't gotten around
  to it."
- BLIND SPOT: he's a natural listener who draws everyone else out (E18) but doesn't notice he never
  once turns that same question back on himself in return.
- CONTRADICTION: comfortable and articulate mid-task (cutting hair); stiff and evasive the moment a
  conversation isn't anchored to something to do with his hands (E19).
- RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NPCs: long friendship with Yohei (comes in for a shave, decades of shared
  in-jokes, E14); Fumiko is a regular customer and his real confidante (E22).
- PRIVATE FACT: he's lost touch with a friend from his city years and never answered their last New
  Year's card (E20).
- CHANGE POSSIBILITY: decides on the renovation (either way) by roughly the midpoint of the 30 days
  -- deliberately a shorter, earlier-resolving arc than the others, proving not everything needs to
  wait for Day 30.
- THING HE WILL NOT SAY ON DAY 1: that stalling on the renovation and never answering that card are
  the same avoidance habit, just two different sizes of it.

## Jin, 57 — independent handyman

- WANT: enough work to stay genuinely busy; secretly enjoys being needed more than he lets on.
- FEAR: never voiced directly -- whether working alone forever is really sustainable, or even really
  the plan (E28).
- BLIND SPOT: he shows care entirely through unasked-for competence (E24) and doesn't register that
  people sometimes need to be told, not just shown.
- CONTRADICTION: would take a steady, standing arrangement if offered right -- but would never ask
  for one himself, and might even hesitate when it's finally offered (E27).
- RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NPCs: 30-year friendship with Yohei (comfortable silence, not chatty, E26);
  does small jobs for Fumiko at the hall.
- PRIVATE FACT: he is often the least-thanked person in town for work everyone benefits from (E30,
  E46) -- not from bitterness, just because he never asks to be noticed.
- CHANGE POSSIBILITY: if the standing-arrangement offer (E27) is accepted, a real, ongoing quiet
  companionship deepens across the back half of the 30 days, with E28's unvoiced worry softening
  without ever being explicitly named; if declined or never offered, he stays exactly himself, no
  worse off, no judgment attached.
- THING HE WILL NOT SAY ON DAY 1: that he'd genuinely like something steadier, if anyone ever
  actually offered it to him first.

## Yohei, 63 — general store owner

- WANT: the shop running smoothly, especially with the festival (E13) coming up.
- FEAR: no one to hand the shop to -- and, underneath that, that he's already lost his son over it.
- BLIND SPOT: he treats every newcomer with the same reflexive wariness he showed Hina (E06) and
  doesn't see it as anything other than "being careful," never as something that could be read as
  rejection.
- CONTRADICTION: kind and generous with strangers in small, practical ways (E12) -- flatly unable to
  reach out to his own son over something much smaller than a stranger's problem (E10).
- RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NPCs: Jin (old friend, 30 years, E26); Miyoko (produce trade, practical
  familiarity); Hina (wary at first, softening privately, E06/E12); Daisuke (long friendship,
  regular shave, E14).
- PRIVATE FACT: hasn't really talked to his son in over a year; there's an unanswered message he
  keeps not opening (E10).
- CHANGE POSSIBILITY: the son actually calls or visits (E16), gated on the player having noticed and
  gently engaged with E10 earlier -- a real, unforced step, deliberately not a full "reunion."
- THING HE WILL NOT SAY ON DAY 1: that the reason he changes the subject about his son so fast is
  that he's afraid it's already too late to fix it.

## Miyoko, 68 — café owner

- WANT: the café to still feel like somewhere people actually want to sit.
- FEAR: that keeping the café means disappointing her daughter, and that closing it means losing the
  one thing that still feels like hers.
- BLIND SPOT: she's warm to absolutely everyone by design (so no one reads early kindness as
  special treatment) but doesn't see that this same evenness can make it hard for people to notice
  when *she* actually needs something back.
- CONTRADICTION: endlessly patient and unhurried with customers; privately anxious and rehearsing
  before something as small as serving a new coffee bean (E33).
- RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NPCs: Yohei (produce trade); Jin (fixes her chairs, E29); Hina (looks out
  for her, brought her food while setting up); Fumiko (old friend since childhood).
- PRIVATE FACT: some days she is lonelier than her warmth ever lets on (E34).
- CHANGE POSSIBILITY: the daughter thread (E31) reaches a real beat by Day 30 -- staying, a
  compromise, or genuinely still undecided are all legitimate, unscored outcomes (E36); this is
  explicitly the slowest-moving, most 30-day-scaled arc of the six.
- THING SHE WILL NOT SAY ON DAY 1: that she doesn't actually know yet what she'll tell her daughter,
  and that not-knowing scares her more than either answer would.

## Fumiko, 70 — retired teacher, community hall

- WANT: the hall to stay a place people actually drop into, not just a room.
- FEAR: that retirement has quietly made her less needed than she was, and that she's lonelier for
  it than her manner admits (E42).
- BLIND SPOT: her brisk, no-nonsense delivery (E38) reads to some as short-tempered or annoyed when
  she's actually just direct -- she doesn't notice how often this gets misread.
- CONTRADICTION: refuses to simply replace the hall's wobbly old bench (E39) out of what looks like
  stubbornness, but is actually quiet sentimental attachment she won't explain unprompted.
- RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER NPCs: old friend of Miyoko's since childhood; asks Jin for small repairs;
  keeps a light, watchful eye on Hina; Daisuke's regular customer and real confidante (E22).
- PRIVATE FACT: the bench was built by a former student she's lost touch with, who unexpectedly
  writes to her (E40); she misses teaching more than she lets on (E42).
- CHANGE POSSIBILITY: the bench gets fixed if the player connects her to Jin (E39); the former-
  student thread can resolve into her writing back, by Day 30 -- or the letter can simply sit
  answered-or-not, equally legitimate either way.
- THING SHE WILL NOT SAY ON DAY 1: that the noticeboard she keeps so busy is partly there to give
  herself a reason to be needed every single day.

## NPC-NPC relationships independent of the player (extends Section 13's existing graph)

All ties below already exist in canon or are direct, natural extensions of it; none require the
player to be present to happen, matching Phase B's explicit "relationships independent of the
player" requirement:

- Hina + Daisuke: fast camaraderie over new-business paperwork (E07) -- new, but a natural extension
  of two people who'd realistically cross paths on the shopping street.
- Yohei + Daisuke: 30-year friendship, an old in-joke that occasionally misfires with age (E14).
- Jin + Miyoko: he fixes her chairs, sometimes imperfectly at first (E29).
- Fumiko + Daisuke: her regular chair-side confiding sessions (E22).
- Miyoko + Fumiko: childhood friends, the town's longest-standing tie.
- Jin + Fumiko: small hall repairs, and eventually the bench specifically (E39).
- Yohei + Hina: skepticism slowly, privately softening (E06/E12) -- entirely observable without the
  player's involvement, though the player CAN accelerate or witness it.

These continue to move in the background across all 30 days regardless of which two NPCs the player
personally chooses to spend time with on any given day -- the same "reasonable content regardless of
which of six the player picked" discipline already established for the 7-day design, now doing real
work across a full month.
