# NEW LIFE — event × NPC matrix V1

Phase 25 design experiment. The rows are the six NPCs; columns point to each complete cell below. “No-player” means what they can initiate if the player never speaks. Their private appraisal is **authoring state, never omniscient player narration**. Event truth is fixed in `NEWLIFE_THREE_EVENT_ENGINE_V1.md`; whether a person sees or learns it can vary.

| NPC | EVENT1: small trial | EVENT2: expectation gap | EVENT3: decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hina | H1 — personal offer, public quantities | H2 — defense → customer evidence | H3 — owns an offer or an opt-out |
| Yohei | Y1 — count versus encouragement | Y2 — warning versus repair | Y3 — narrow guarantee or solo stock |
| Daisuke | D1 — chair repair versus venue promise | D2 — joke runs out; workshop choice | D3 — explicit yes/no |
| Jin | J1 — safe bounded setup | J2 — work limit and a specific request | J3 — paid task or refusal |
| Miyoko | M1 — hospitality with seat cost | M2 — prioritizes café customers | M3 — states seat boundary |
| Fumiko | F1 — formally approved notice | F2 — corrects a materially misleading notice | F3 — delegates sign ownership or pauses |

## Hina

**H1 / EVENT1:** INITIAL_REACTION excited and uneasy at first public sale. PUBLIC_ACTION posts two prices and “本日30点”, holds 12 reservations, sells 18 at the counter. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL “a tiny offer may look unserious.” WHO_THEY_APPROACH Miyoko for honest tasting, Daisuke for a table. WHO_THEY_AVOID Yohei immediately after his question. WHAT_THEY_SAY “スコーン20個280円、クッキー10袋240円。12点は予約、店頭は18点です” if asked; also admits she has no pickup helper. WHAT_THEY_DO_NOT_SAY she fears a reduced offer is judged as failure. STATE_CHANGE_POSSIBILITY prices/counts become discussable facts; defensiveness rises if corrected in front of customers. PLAYER_INTERVENTION_OPPORTUNITY ask exact number, point to missing reservation wording, offer a bounded pickup task. NO-PLAYER_OUTCOME Miyoko asks her how many are reserved; she answers, but leaves the first draft posted until a later correction.

**H2 / EVENT2:** INITIAL_REACTION tells the visitor “30 total”, too quickly. PUBLIC_ACTION pauses sales long enough to read the old photo and compare the reservation tally. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL she first hears a complaint as rejection of her product. APPROACH Miyoko for what customer actually said, eventually Yohei for counts. AVOID Fumiko while still angry about who approved the sign. SAYS “見せ方が違った。今は店頭分が何点か書き直します” if she updates; before that, “合計では30です”. UNSAID “I skipped the unglamorous part.” CHANGE may update belief that product quality alone is enough, or double down and avoid the sign. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY show visitor wording without publicly shaming her; take a specific pickup role with consent. NO-PLAYER Miyoko repeats the customer's exact misunderstanding; Hina may correct the counter label herself even if still defensive.

**H3 / EVENT3:** INITIAL_REACTION wants a second chance but not another promise she cannot serve. PUBLIC_ACTION chooses limited repeat, a smaller independent offer, or pause. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL doubts whether she can own both craft and service. APPROACH Fumiko for a truthful notice and Miyoko for a critique. AVOID Yohei if E2 stayed accusatory. SAYS “次は予約と店頭を別に出す。それでも私の店で決めたい”. UNSAID approval from everybody still matters to her. CHANGE accepts evidence and makes her own bounded promise, or stays proud and solo. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY ask about costs and who handles pickup, with no power to vote for her. NO-PLAYER proposes her own small-scale test if roles are clear; otherwise independently sells through her shop without the collective project.

## Yohei

**Y1 / EVENT1:** INITIAL_REACTION doubts the sign, not the scones. PUBLIC_ACTION compares “30” with the reservation sheet; keeps his shop open. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL expects he may be blamed if buyers feel misled. APPROACH Daisuke for a second reading; Hina once with a precise question. AVOID Fumiko's enthusiastic planning. SAYS “店頭は何点だ”; UNSAID “I don't want the newcomer embarrassed.” CHANGE H→Y friction might increase even as Y→H competence respect rises if she answers. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY ask him for the count or challenge his tone privately. NO-PLAYER leaves Hina a written count suggestion, then returns to his own customers.

**Y2 / EVENT2:** INITIAL_REACTION thinks “I warned them”, then sees an affected visitor. PUBLIC_ACTION offers the dated reservation tally, labels held items separately if invited. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL competence feels vindicated but his method failed to prevent harm. APPROACH Hina if she seeks a correction, Daisuke if she refuses. AVOID speaking to visitor on Hina's behalf without consent. SAYS “数字は合う。見た人への約束が違う”. UNSAID a correct warning delivered badly is partly his responsibility as a neighbor. CHANGE from “warning ends my duty” toward practical repair, or entrenches certainty. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY invite him to help with a stock board, without making him guarantor. NO-PLAYER marks his own shelf accurately and can give Hina the count after she asks; he does not rescue the project automatically.

**Y3 / EVENT3:** INITIAL_REACTION wants explicit scope before agreement. PUBLIC_ACTION accepts only his own stock role or opts out. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL renewed trust may coexist with irritation. APPROACH Daisuke to hear if venue is real; Hina to verify quantities. AVOID Fumiko's open-ended duty list. SAYS “うちの分なら数を出す。他の店の予約は持たん”. UNSAID he would like the smaller joint plan to work. CHANGE revises one inference about Hina, not his conscientiousness. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY request a narrow commitment with deadline. NO-PLAYER states a narrow offer if conditions are met; otherwise refuses with no collapse of the town.

## Daisuke

**D1 / EVENT1:** INITIAL_REACTION enjoys an excuse to fix something. PUBLIC_ACTION repairs a queue chair; offers a joke, not a workshop booking. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL “If I say yes now, they will keep using my place.” APPROACH Yohei as old friend and Jin over the table. AVOID Fumiko's booking sheet. SAYS “椅子なら今日直る。店の予定は…ちょっと待って”. UNSAID he may actually prefer a defined small role. CHANGE group trust can drop from the delay while friendliness stays. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY ask only for the date of his venue answer, not his motives. NO-PLAYER fixes the chair and leaves workshop undecided.

**D2 / EVENT2:** INITIAL_REACTION starts with a joke that nobody follows. PUBLIC_ACTION gives a definitive yes for one hour or no for the workshop after Fumiko's cutoff. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL the delay now has another person's cost. APPROACH Yohei for a blunt check, Jin for physical feasibility. AVOID Hina when she is defending the sign. SAYS “椅子は直せる。工房は今回は貸せない” or “一時間だけ使っていい”. UNSAID disappointment in himself if he delays again. CHANGE avoidance → decision can occur without player; if he evades again, the venue option expires. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY point out the deadline and accept either answer. NO-PLAYER Yohei asks him plainly; he answers or loses the booking.

**D3 / EVENT3:** INITIAL_REACTION relieved if a no is respected, anxious if a yes became vague. PUBLIC_ACTION signs up for exactly the chosen use, or sits out with a chair repair only. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL learns that a narrow no can preserve friendship. APPROACH Fumiko to set a time; Yohei for a joke afterward. AVOID open-ended “we'll see”. SAYS “今決めた。ここまではやる”. UNSAID he still dislikes planning. CHANGE may own decision, not become a leader. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY ask for a specific commitment and do not treat a no as failure. NO-PLAYER writes down his answer himself; if he still dodges, Fumiko chooses another location.

## Jin

**J1 / EVENT1:** INITIAL_REACTION checks the load before reading the flyer. PUBLIC_ACTION positions tables safely for the agreed window. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL a successful setup will lead to unpriced extra requests. APPROACH Miyoko for access to the passage, Fumiko for agreed closing time. AVOID Daisuke's improvised fix until it is safe. SAYS “この台はここ。先に通り道を空ける”. UNSAID he would accept help carrying one heavy piece. CHANGE F→J trust can improve only if schedule respected. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY offer a single lift after asking. NO-PLAYER completes his contract and leaves before café overflow makes him a default porter.

**J2 / EVENT2:** INITIAL_REACTION sees a blocked path, not a reputation crisis. PUBLIC_ACTION clears immediate obstruction, then stops at contract limit. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL dislikes being seen struggling. APPROACH Miyoko for a cart key; Daisuke if one chair must move. AVOID Fumiko's “just one more” until a limit is written. SAYS “この一往復、手を貸してくれるか” if he asks; otherwise “今日はここまで”. UNSAID a generic “you're amazing” does not help. CHANGE toward a bounded request or regression into solo overtime and a later refusal. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY help with one explicitly permitted task, no unsanctioned rescue. NO-PLAYER asks Miyoko for one cart or completes only what is safe, with consequences visible to others.

**J3 / EVENT3:** INITIAL_REACTION cares about exact task and paid hours. PUBLIC_ACTION accepts a defined setup or opts out. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL his limits can be respected without loss of usefulness. APPROACH Fumiko with written scope, Miyoko for passage access. AVOID indefinite labor list. SAYS “二時間ならやる。受け渡し係は別だ”. UNSAID he appreciates being asked beforehand. CHANGE receiving help does not require increased talkativeness. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY respect his decline and name the labor cost. NO-PLAYER chooses his own workload; another provider or a smaller layout is possible.

## Miyoko

**M1 / EVENT1:** INITIAL_REACTION likes Hina's effort, checks four café chairs. PUBLIC_ACTION serves paid coffee and offers an honest tasting note. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL fears a boundary will look unwelcoming. APPROACH Jin about passage, Hina about flavor. AVOID Fumiko's assumption that all seats are communal. SAYS “味はいい。並ぶ場所は喫茶の外にしてね” if she names a limit. UNSAID she needs her own customers to be able to sit. CHANGE may discover she can be helpful without giving away all space. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY ask what café can actually offer rather than promise its seats. NO-PLAYER moves one sign to protect a customer table but initially leaves the larger misunderstanding open.

**M2 / EVENT2:** INITIAL_REACTION notices visitors spilling into café seating. PUBLIC_ACTION finishes existing café orders before responding to overflow. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL she is angry at an assumption, not the visitor. APPROACH Fumiko directly, Jin about safe layout. AVOID telling Hina her product caused everything. SAYS “ごめんなさい、ここは喫茶のお客さんの席です。待つなら会館側へ”. UNSAID “I said yes too vaguely yesterday.” CHANGE toward own voice; regression if she smiles and silently absorbs it. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY support her stated boundary, not speak for her. NO-PLAYER independently turns away overflow kindly and tells Fumiko why.

**M3 / EVENT3:** INITIAL_REACTION wants a reason to stay connected without losing her room. PUBLIC_ACTION states exact coffee/seating availability or opts out of the next trial. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL people may like her even after a no. APPROACH Hina with commercial critique, Fumiko for independent scheduling. AVOID Daisuke's jokes if they obscure capacity. SAYS “手伝うなら飲み物まで。待合にはしない”. UNSAID she worries about losing the street's closeness. CHANGE warmth plus limit, or continuing depletion. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY ask permission and accept a refusal. NO-PLAYER makes her own café plan, joins conditionally or serves coffee separately.

## Fumiko

**F1 / EVENT1:** INITIAL_REACTION sees a workable date and an incomplete responsibility list. PUBLIC_ACTION approves and posts “本日30点”. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL a formal check will protect clarity. APPROACH Hina for wording, Miyoko for what she believes is seating agreement. AVOID Yohei's tone while retaining his concern. SAYS “掲示は済んだ。受け渡しの担当は?” UNSAID she has not read the sign as a first-time visitor. CHANGE paper approval can increase false confidence. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY read the sign literally and ask who edits it. NO-PLAYER posts it; independently writes a pickup question on her sheet but doesn't resolve it in time.

**F2 / EVENT2:** INITIAL_REACTION first cites approved wording. PUBLIC_ACTION dates a correction, removes outdated sign from hall, and identifies who informs misled visitors if she accepts fault. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL her process was followed and still failed. APPROACH Miyoko to hear the actual seat burden; Yohei for counts. AVOID Hina until both calm down. SAYS “私が通した掲示です。予約12、店頭18と書き直す”. UNSAID she is embarrassed that a novice visitor saw more than she did. CHANGE control → visible correction with ownership, or defensive suspension of all signs. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY show the confusing photo, no unauthorized alteration of her official notice. NO-PLAYER Miyoko tells her about actual customers; Fumiko can correct the notice by herself.

**F3 / EVENT3:** INITIAL_REACTION wants a rule she can enforce fairly. PUBLIC_ACTION offers a hall slot only with a named editor/cutoff, or pauses collective announcements. PRIVATE_APPRAISAL sharing responsibility need not mean abandoning it. APPROACH Hina for sale facts, Miyoko for seating consent, Jin for working hours. AVOID generic unanimous declarations. SAYS “掲示担当は一人。変更は誰がいつ出す?” UNSAID she would enjoy someone else taking a fair share. CHANGE bounded delegation or narrower control. PLAYER_OPPORTUNITY volunteer for a defined task subject to their consent, not cast the deciding vote. NO-PLAYER calls a meeting and makes a limited offer or pauses postings on her own authority.

## Diversity check at the pressure peak

E2 yields six different first acts: **H explains**, **Y presents a count**, **D jokes then decides**, **J moves a physical obstruction**, **M protects café customers**, **F checks the approved notice**. Each can act, change, resist or leave a conflict unresolved without player permission. No cell relies on “trust +1 → reveals secret.”
