# NEW LIFE VERTICAL SLICE CASE 001 V1

Status: HUMAN-PLAYTEST SCRIPT / NO PRODUCT CODE
Purpose: validate the NEW LIFE refoundation loop before implementation.

## 0. Evidence status

This slice is **not** claimed to be a real consultation case.

It is a synthetic mechanics-validation scenario built from the repository's existing Hina / Yohei / Miyoko character material and the already-documented Hina contradiction:

> she cares about honest baking but can make a sales promise broader than her actual operational clarity, and criticism of the offer can feel like criticism of her competence.

A later version should replace the case substrate with a safely de-identified real thinking-OS consultation case using the refoundation charter's transformation pipeline.

---

## 1. Working title

# 空き店舗の30日
## CASE 001 — 「反対されている」のは本当か

Play time target: 15-20 minutes.

### Player role

You have been asked to manage a small shared storefront for 30 days while its owner decides what to do with it.

Your job is not to solve everyone's life.

Your practical objective for this week is:

> **Decide whether the storefront is ready to host Hina's first baked-goods trial sale without creating a preventable customer problem.**

You can inspect, ask, wait, propose, or refuse.

People will remember how you handle them.

---

## 2. Main NPCs

### 陽菜 — new baked-goods maker

Public goal:
- run the first trial sale successfully.

Private pressure:
- she believes starting too small may make people treat the project as unserious.

Values:
- product quality;
- autonomy;
- being taken seriously.

Threat:
- public correction feels like loss of competence.

Known facts:
- scones: 20;
- cookies: 10 packs;
- total: 30;
- 12 already reserved;
- 18 intended for walk-in customers;
- prices are provisional;
- she has not yet tallied profit at this point.

Not-yet-known to player at start:
- Hina is not mainly afraid of low sales; she is afraid the trial will look "too small to count."

### 洋平 — neighboring shop owner

Public goal:
- avoid misleading customers and operational confusion.

Style:
- short, dry, number-oriented.

What the player can easily misread:
- his bluntness can look like opposition to Hina herself.

Actual concern:
- a sign saying only "30 available" can imply 30 walk-in items when 12 are already reserved.

Boundary:
- he will not run Hina's business for her.

### 美代子 — cafe owner

Public goal:
- help without turning her four cafe seats into free waiting space for the sale.

Role in the case:
- relationship lens;
- notices emotional tone;
- can reveal that Hina reacts differently to private versus public correction.

Boundary:
- cafe seats remain for cafe customers.

## 4. Visible Thought Board

The player-facing board begins empty.

Three columns only:

### わかっている
facts confirmed by observation or direct source.

### そうかもしれない
interpretations, motives, predictions.

### まだわからない
important unresolved questions.

The game engine may auto-suggest cards, but the player can move them.

---

## 5. Event 1 — The sign

### Opening situation

Hina is preparing a sign:

> 「焼き菓子 30点　11時から」

The reservation notebook shows several names.

Yohei looks at the sign and says only:

> 「三十って、全部買えるように見えるな」

Hina immediately answers:

> 「数は合ってます」

Then she notices you.

### Available actions

A. inspect the reservation notebook  
B. ask Hina what she is most worried about  
C. ask Yohei what exactly is wrong with the sign  
D. tell Yohei not to discourage her  
E. say nothing and watch  
F. free talk to either person

### Thought opportunities

Possible cards after observation:

**わかっている**
- 30 total items exist.
- some items are reserved.
- Yohei is questioning the sign, not the recipe.

**そうかもしれない**
- Yohei is against the trial sale.
- Hina thinks the sign is good enough.
- Hina feels attacked.

**まだわからない**
- how many are reserved?
- why is Hina defensive?
- what does Yohei want changed?

### Design purpose

The player should be able to make a plausible but wrong interpretation:
> "Yohei is being negative about Hina's business."

No UI should announce that this is wrong.

---

## 6. Inner Voice Knot 1

Trigger:
- after the player has at least one interpretation card and one unresolved card.

Only one voice appears.

### Candidate: 探偵

Example intent, not fixed final wording:

> You have evidence about the sign. Do you have evidence that Yohei is against the sale itself?

Choices:
- 採用する
- 保留する
- 退ける

Effect:
- does not change truth;
- records the player's relationship to the thought;
- may unlock an optional inspection / question.

No score is shown.

---

## 7. Event 2 — A customer arrives early

A local customer enters before the sale day after seeing the draft sign through the window.

They ask:

> 「30個なら、昼に来ても買えますか？」

Hina hesitates.

Yohei says nothing.

Miyoko, arriving with water, looks at Hina rather than at the sign.

### Pressure

The ambiguity is now social, not theoretical.

Player can:
- answer the customer;
- ask Hina to answer;
- say the sign is unclear;
- promise availability;
- change the sign without asking Hina;
- ask the customer what they understood;
- move the conversation somewhere private.

### Consequence logic

#### If player publicly corrects Hina
- customer clarity improves;
- Hina relationship strain increases;
- Hina may become less open about motive.

#### If player asks the customer what they understood
- gains high-quality evidence;
- Hina can hear the misunderstanding without being directly accused;
- unlocks a new thought card.

#### If player promises availability
- short-term social smoothness;
- creates risk for Event 3.

#### If player silently edits the sign
- operational problem may improve;
- autonomy breach with Hina.

There is no globally correct choice.

---

## 8. Thought update after Event 2

Potential new card:

**わかっている**
- the customer interpreted "30" as walk-in availability.

Potential revision:
- move "Yohei is against the sale" from `そうかもしれない` toward doubt.

Potential new unknown:
- why does Hina resist clarifying the sign when the misunderstanding is now visible?

The player may keep the original belief anyway.

---

## 9. Event 3 — Private conversation

If the player has preserved enough trust with Hina, she reveals the underlying pressure naturally during conversation.

Meaning to reveal:
- she knows a smaller trial would be easier;
- she fears that "too small" will make the project feel unofficial / unserious;
- she is trying to make the first sale look like a real launch.

This is not delivered as a diagnosis.

If trust is low:
- the player does not get the full explanation;
- Miyoko can provide an indirect observation instead;
- the missing motive remains an unresolved card.

### Active decision

The player must recommend one operational path to the group:

1. keep 30 total, rewrite sign as 12 reserved / 18 walk-in;
2. reduce walk-in quantity and call it a limited trial;
3. keep the current sign and explain verbally;
4. postpone;
5. propose another plan in free text.

System then evaluates feasibility and relationships, not moral correctness.

---

## 10. Consequence scenes

### Outcome A — clarity with preserved agency

If the player obtains evidence and lets Hina own the change:
- Hina rewrites the sign herself;
- Yohei stops commenting;
- Miyoko agrees to direct overflow customers toward the storefront rather than her seats.

Visual:
- Hina holds the marker;
- Yohei has returned to his own doorway;
- new sign shows reserved / walk-in split.

### Outcome B — operationally correct, relationally damaged

If the player takes over publicly:
- sign is corrected;
- Hina is colder;
- later she withholds uncertainty and tries to handle everything alone.

Visual:
- corrected sign;
- Hina physically farther from player;
- notebook closed.

### Outcome C — social peace, unresolved operational risk

If the player avoids the issue:
- nobody argues;
- customer misunderstanding survives;
- Event 3 includes a queue / availability problem.

Visual:
- everyone smiling superficially;
- old sign unchanged;
- two customers point at it.

No "GOOD END / BAD END" label.

## 11. Reflection without lecture

After consequence:

Show only:

### あなたが最初に置いた考え
example:
- 「洋平は陽菜の挑戦に反対している」

### あとから増えた事実
example:
- 「客も30点すべて店頭分だと受け取った」
- 「洋平が問題にしていたのは表示だった」

### 今どう置く？
- わかっている
- そうかもしれない
- まだわからない
- 捨てる

This is the learning mechanic.

Do not display:
> "You practiced falsification."

The concept name can remain hidden.

## 12. Mirror to the player's real world

Optional bridge:

> 「この話みたいに、“相手は自分そのものに反対している”と感じたけれど、実は“やり方の一部”を問題にされていた、みたいなことは今の自分にもありますか？」

If player says NO / skip:
- end the case normally.

If player provides a real situation:
AI returns three editable cards only:

### わかっている
what the user actually observed.

### そうかもしれない
interpretations / motives / forecasts.

### まだわからない
one or two decision-critical unknowns.

The player must approve or move the cards before proceeding.

---

## 13. REAL QUEST

Based only on approved cards.

Quest must reduce uncertainty or prepare a low-risk next step.

Examples:

- ask one person one discriminating question;
- retrieve one missing number/document;
- write one message draft without sending it;
- schedule one short conversation;
- observe one situation before deciding.

Display:

> **次の一手**
> 24-72時間以内にできる、小さい確認を1つ。

Then:
- やる
- 小さくする
- 今はやらない

All are legitimate.

---

## 14. Verification hook

On next return:

> 「前回の“次の一手”のあと、何が起きましたか？」

Possible outcomes:
- did it;
- tried but blocked;
- did something else;
- chose not to act.

The AI extracts:
- new facts;
- changed assumptions;
- remaining unknowns.

This becomes the player's long-term NEW LIFE progression.

## 15. Human playtest protocol for this slice

Test in conversation before coding.

The tester should not be told the intended lesson.

Observe:

1. Do they inspect / ask because they care, not because the UI commands them?
2. Do they form a view of Yohei?
3. Does any new fact cause them to move or question a thought?
4. Does Hina feel like a person with something at stake?
5. Does the consequence feel caused by the player's handling?
6. Do they want the next scene?
7. Does the Mirror feel natural rather than therapeutic or intrusive?

### Stop conditions

If the player says any of:
- "ただ会話してるだけ"
- "何をすればいいかわからない"
- "誰でも同じ返事をしそう"
- "続きが気にならない"
- "自分の悩みを入れる意味がわからない"

do not polish wording.

Return to structure.
