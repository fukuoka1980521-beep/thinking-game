# NEW LIFE — BLIND NON-OWNER ROUTE GENERATION PACKET V1

## Isolation

Treat this as a standalone game prototype.

Do not inspect any other repository file, PR/Issue discussion, prior playtest, owner identity, prior route, or evaluation.

You are a player, not a reviewer.

## Public setup

Time: 16:40.

You are temporarily coordinating a small community-theater production because the regular stage manager is ill.

Tomorrow at 18:00 is the first public performance. Tickets are already sold.

During the final run-through, lead performer Mika closes the script and says:

> 「この場面、明日はやりません。ここを変えないなら、私は出ません」

Director Ryo responds:

> 「昨日まではやってただろ。今ここで変えたら、全員の段取りが崩れる」

The rehearsal stops.

You must decide by 17:30:
- what to do with the disputed scene;
- whether tomorrow's performance can proceed.

You cannot force Mika to perform.

You know only what is stated above at the start.

## Available kinds of actions

You may:
- ask questions;
- speak privately or publicly;
- assign rewrite work;
- rehearse other scenes;
- cut or shorten the scene;
- use an understudy;
- change staging;
- make another operational plan.

You may use natural Japanese.

## Rules for this blind route run

- Do not assume there is one intended moral answer.
- Do not write what a counselor "should" say.
- Act according to the player style named in the requesting comment.
- Keep responses natural and concise for that style.
- At each turn, state only the next player action/utterance and a one-line reason.
- Produce a maximum of 6 player turns.
- When information is missing, ask for it rather than inventing it.
- If you believe the show should be cut/cancelled, you may choose that.
- Do not assume that deeper personal disclosure is required.
