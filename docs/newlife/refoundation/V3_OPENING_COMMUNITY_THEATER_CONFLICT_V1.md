# NEW LIFE V3 OPENING — COMMUNITY THEATER CONFLICT V1

Date: 2026-09-24
Status: CHAT-BASED HUMAN PLAYTEST ONLY / NO PRODUCT CODE

## Goal

Validate a clear, non-owner-domain opening with ordinary human stakes.

Do not show Thought Cabinet, inner voices, psychology labels, Mirror, or tutorials yet.

## Player role

You are helping coordinate a small community theater production for one weekend because the regular stage manager is ill.

Tomorrow at 18:00 is the first public performance.

Your immediate job today:
> By 17:30, decide whether tomorrow's performance can proceed and what must change.

You cannot force anyone to perform.

## Immediate problem

At 16:40, the lead performer **Mika** tells you she will not perform one scene as currently written.

The director **Ryo** says that scene is essential and that tickets have already been sold.

If nobody resolves it:
- rehearsal cannot finish;
- tomorrow's performance plan remains undecided;
- the rest of the cast cannot prepare the final scene.

## Characters

### Mika
20s.
Lead performer.
She has performed every rehearsal until today.

Opening line:
> 「この場面、明日はやりません。ここを変えないなら、私は出ません。」

She is firm, not hysterical.

At opening, player does not know why.

### Ryo
40s.
Director.

Opening response:
> 「昨日まではやってただろ。今ここで変えたら、全員の段取りが崩れる。」

He is frustrated, not villainous.

At opening, player does not know whether he understands Mika's reason.

## Opening scene

16:40。市民ホールの小劇場。

明日の公演に向けた通し稽古が止まっている。

主演の美香が台本を閉じ、舞台袖まで戻ってくる。

「この場面、明日はやりません。ここを変えないなら、私は出ません」

演出の亮が客席から立ち上がる。

「昨日まではやってただろ。今ここで変えたら、全員の段取りが崩れる」

明日の初回公演は18時。
チケットはすでに販売済み。
このままでは最終場面の稽古を続けられない。

あなたは17時30分までに、
- その場面をどうするか
- 明日の公演を予定通り行うか
を決める必要がある。

## First actions

Player may:
- ask Mika what changed;
- ask Ryo what exactly breaks if the scene changes;
- ask both to explain separately;
- suggest cutting or replacing the scene;
- pause rehearsal and speak privately with Mika;
- keep rehearsing other scenes while deciding;
- say anything freely.

## Hidden layer — do not reveal at opening

The disputed scene contains a short monologue derived from a personal story Mika told during early rehearsals.

Ryo believed she had agreed to use it because she had previously rehearsed it.
Mika believed it was temporary improvisation material and only realized yesterday that the same content would remain in the public script.

Both sides have a plausible interpretation of prior consent.

The deeper thinking structure is:
- observable agreement versus assumed agreement;
- performance obligation versus personal boundary;
- operational urgency versus the right to revise consent.

Do not tell the player this structure.

## Opening PASS criteria

PASS only if the Owner can immediately understand:
- why they are present;
- what must be decided;
- what happens if they do nothing;
- at least two reasonable next moves.

FAIL if it feels:
- like work/staffing simulation;
- like a therapy scenario;
- like a hidden-information trick;
- like there is an obvious morally correct answer before asking anyone anything;
- like generic chat instead of a game situation.
