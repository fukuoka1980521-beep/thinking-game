# NEW LIFE V2 OPENING — WORK STAFFING CONFLICT V1

Date: 2026-09-24
Status: CHAT-BASED HUMAN PLAYTEST ONLY / NO PRODUCT CODE

## 0. Purpose

This V2 opening exists only to test whether the player can understand the situation and care enough to act.

Do not introduce:
- Thought Cabinet;
- inner voices;
- psychology labels;
- hidden clue objects;
- real-life Mirror;
- long exposition.

Those mechanics are locked until the opening itself is humanly coherent.

The first 5 minutes must stand on ordinary human stakes.

---

## 1. Opening gate

Within 30 seconds the player must understand:

### Why am I here?
You are covering as the temporary site coordinator today.

### What is the immediate problem?
Tomorrow morning's job needs three workers. One of the three says he cannot come and wants to quit.

### What happens if nobody acts?
The team will be one person short for a customer job starting at 8:30 tomorrow.

### What can I actually do?
You can talk to the worker, talk to the coworker, change the staffing plan, reduce or reschedule the job, or call the customer.

No hidden knowledge is required to understand the conflict.

## 3. Working title

# 明日の三人目

Target opening playtime: 5 minutes.
Full slice target: 15–20 minutes.

---

## 4. Player role

You are helping manage a small installation / delivery crew for one day because the usual coordinator is absent.

It is 16:10.

Tomorrow:
- customer arrival: 8:30
- required crew: 3 people
- currently assigned: Takumi, Sota, Kenta
- equipment booking and customer time are already fixed.

Your immediate responsibility:

> Before 17:00, make a workable plan for tomorrow and tell the crew what to do.

You are not the company owner.
You can reorganize the job and talk to the customer, but you cannot force anyone to work.

---

## 5. Characters

### 拓海 / Takumi

20s.
Has worked with the crew for about 8 months.

At 16:10 he tells the player:

> 「すみません。明日の現場、行けません。……あと、今月で辞めたいです。」

He is not crying, angry, or visibly injured.
He has already packed his own work gloves into his bag.

What the player does NOT know yet:
- whether this is impulsive;
- whether something happened today;
- whether he has another job;
- whether there is conflict with someone;
- whether he is simply exhausted.

Do not signal the answer.

### 壮太 / Sota

30s.
Experienced worker.
Tomorrow's job leader.

Immediate concern:
- 3 people were planned for a reason;
- if they arrive with 2, setup may run late.

His first response should be natural and practical, not villainous.

Example:

> 「え、明日？　今言うのか。二人じゃ朝の搬入きついぞ。」

He is irritated, but the scene must not tell the player whether his irritation is fair or unfair.

### 健太 / Kenta

Present only by name in the first 5 minutes.
Already confirmed for tomorrow.

Do not add a third talking NPC yet.

## 6. Opening scene

### Scene text

16:10。明日の現場の確認をしていると、拓海が作業手袋をバッグにしまってから、あなたのところへ来る。

「すみません。明日の現場、行けません。……あと、今月で辞めたいです」

隣で明日の資材表を見ていた壮太が顔を上げる。

「え、明日？　今言うのか。二人じゃ朝の搬入きついぞ」

明日の客先は8時30分開始。
もともとの予定は3人。
今のままなら1人足りない。

17時までに、明日の動きを決めて二人へ伝える必要がある。

### What is deliberately NOT shown yet

No:
- diagnosis;
- motive;
- mysterious object;
- thought cards;
- "which one is fact/interpretation?";
- tutorial;
- narrator telling the player what to think.

## 7. First player action

The player may:

1. ask Takumi: 「明日だけ無理なのか、辞めたい話と同じ理由なの？」
2. ask Sota why three people are necessary;
3. ask Takumi what happened;
4. start looking for a replacement worker;
5. call the customer and discuss timing;
6. propose reducing tomorrow's scope;
7. say something freely.

The game must accept natural free text.

Do not force the player to select from the list if they want to speak naturally.

---

## 8. Conversation behavior rules

### Takumi

If asked an open question like:
- 「何があった？」
- 「どうした？」
- 「辞めたい理由は？」

He should not dump his entire biography.

First-layer answer:

> 「今日だけの話じゃないです。ちょっと前から考えてました。」

If asked:
- 「明日だけ出られない？」
- 「明日は何か予定ある？」

He should answer the actual question first.

Example:
> 「明日は無理です。そこは変えられません。」

Then, depending on tone:
> 「辞める話は……ちゃんと話します。」

If accused:
> 「急すぎる」
or
> 「無責任じゃない？」

He should react as a person:
- become guarded;
- give less information;
- not automatically apologize and reveal everything.

### Sota

If asked why three are needed:

> 「搬入口が狭いから、二人が中で組んで、一人は外で資材回す予定だった。二人でも不可能じゃないけど、客を待たせる可能性は上がる。」

This creates a real trade-off:
- three is preferred;
- two is not literally impossible.

If asked what he thinks about Takumi quitting:

> 「辞めるのは本人の勝手だろ。でも明日の段取りは別だ。」

This is designed to prevent the player from being forced into "Sota is bad / Takumi is victim."

---

## 9. First hidden layer

Only after the player has acted should deeper conflict appear.

Potential substrate for V2:
- Takumi feels he keeps being asked to "just help this time" on days he had not agreed to;
- he has not clearly voiced the boundary before;
- Sota believes Takumi had agreed to the team's flexible scheduling when he joined;
- both have evidence supporting their own interpretation.

This is NOT revealed at the opening.

The design goal is:
- surface issue = tomorrow is short-staffed;
- deeper issue = conflicting expectations around obligation and consent;
- transferable thinking pattern = "operational problem" and "relationship/motive judgment" are not the same question.

---

## 10. First 5-minute success criteria

PASS only if the Owner says or demonstrates:

- the situation is understandable without explanation;
- the dialogue sounds like humans in a real work setting;
- there is an obvious reason to do something;
- more than one reasonable action exists;
- they want to ask at least one question or make one decision.

FAIL if the Owner says:

- "意味がわからない"
- "何をすればいいかわからない"
- "会話が不自然"
- "最初から答えが見えている"
- "教材っぽい"
- "ただの相談チャット"

If FAIL:
do not proceed to hidden layer, Thought Cabinet, or implementation.
