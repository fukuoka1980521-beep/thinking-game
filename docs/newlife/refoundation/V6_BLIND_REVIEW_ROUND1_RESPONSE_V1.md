## 1. State model revised

The previous model used:
- SITUATION
- TRUST
- CLARITY

V6 uses five distinct state concepts.

### SITUATION
Can the practical case still be completed?

### TRUST
Will the NPC continue to communicate and cooperate?

### CLARITY
Does the player understand the actual decision-relevant conflict?

### BOUNDARY
A non-numeric state:
- UNKNOWN
- STATED
- RESPECTED
- OVERRIDDEN

This prevents continued cooperation from being mistaken for consent.

### TIME
A real resource measured in remaining minutes.

Conversation, rewriting, rehearsal, investigation, and recovery all consume time.

This prevents "more conversation" from being automatically superior.

## 2. No single best communication personality

The case must support at least four competent routes:

### Route F — Facilitative
Strength:
- trust;
- richer disclosure;
- may earn reusable thought tools.

Cost:
- time.

Failure mode:
- keeps exploring after enough information exists;
- misses the operational deadline.

### Route M — Managerial / concise
Strength:
- fast;
- can protect both schedule and boundary with direct task framing.

Example:
> 「本人の体験そのままは使わない。物語上必要な機能だけ残して15分で書き直そう。美香、完成したら公開してよい内容かだけ確認して」

This can reach a fully workable resolution without deep personal exploration.

### Route A — Analytical
Strength:
- separates required story function from disputed source material.

Example:
> 「残す必要があるのはどこ？　本人の実話じゃないと成立しない部分はある？」

This can discover the same rewrite seam through problem analysis rather than empathy.

### Route D — Direct
Strength:
- surfaces a hard constraint quickly.

Example:
> 「美香、出演そのものが無理なのか、この実話を使うことだけが無理なのか。そこだけ先に決めよう」

This can reach full clarity with short language.

Risk:
- if delivered publicly after a boundary was already stated, trust may fall.

No route is globally best.

## 3. Outcome descriptors revised

Do not present one outcome as the golden ending.

A case ending is described by what actually happened.

Examples:

### SHOW_PROCEEDS / BOUNDARY_RESPECTED / PERSONAL_PATTERN_UNEXPLORED
The show is saved.
Mika's boundary is respected.
Her broader communication difficulty is left for another time.

This is a valid success.

### SHOW_PROCEEDS / TRUST_HIGH / TIME_COST_HIGH
The show is saved.
The relationship is strong.
The process consumed much of the rehearsal window.

Also valid, with a different trade-off.

### SHOW_PROCEEDS / TRUST_LOW
The show happens.
The practical task succeeds.
Mika leaves the troupe afterward.

Not "wrong"; it is a consequential result.

### SHOW_CHANGED / ARTISTIC_COST_ACCEPTED
The disputed scene is cut.
The show proceeds in a weaker form.
No one is coerced.

This may be the rational result when time is nearly gone.

### SHOW_CANCELLED
The production cannot proceed as planned.

The causal chain must be shown.

## 5. Breakdown must result from a decision, not a tone

### Breakdown example A — false commitment

Player tells Ryo publicly:
> 「昨日までやってたんだから、とりあえずこのまま行こう。美香も大丈夫だよね」

Without getting agreement, the player orders rehearsal to continue.

Consequences:
- Boundary becomes OVERRIDDEN
- Trust falls
- 12 minutes consumed rehearsing unchanged material
- Mika leaves the rehearsal
- rewrite starts too late
- Situation becomes critical

This fails because the player made an unsupported commitment and spent scarce time on a plan that required consent they did not have.

Not because the wording was insufficiently warm.

### Breakdown example B — endless facilitation

Player keeps exploring Mika's feelings and troupe history for 35 minutes before assigning anyone to rewrite or rehearse alternatives.

Consequences:
- Trust rises
- Clarity may rise
- Time collapses
- no revised script is ready
- show plan becomes unstable

A highly empathetic approach can fail.

### Breakdown example C — avoidant delay

Player moves on to other scenes and says:
> 「あとで考えよう」

No owner for the disputed scene is assigned.

At 17:20 the conflict is unchanged.

Failure is caused by deferred decision ownership.

## 6. Recovery mechanics

Poor decisions can be recovered from if enough time remains.

Example after overriding Mika:

Player:
> 「今の決め方はまずかった。公開していいと確認してないのに進めた。止める。実話は使わない前提で作り直す」

Effects:
- Boundary returns from OVERRIDDEN to STATED, not immediately RESPECTED
- Trust partially recovers
- time cost remains
- player must accept a reduced/cut scene if full rewrite is no longer feasible

Recovery restores options, not erased consequences.

## 7. Thought tools become real mechanics

Thought tools are not special phrases and do not gate correctness.

A player can always perform the underlying reasoning unaided.

Owning a tool provides a gameplay advantage: lower time/uncertainty cost.

### Tool: 「友達ならどう扱う？」

Function:
- once per case, request an alternate-person perspective without spending a full conversation turn;
- adds one alternative interpretation to the board;
- costs 0 in-world minutes.

### Tool: 「どこまでなら大丈夫？」

Function:
- when an NPC has objected, opens a compact boundary check:
  - NO
  - OK
  - SUBSTITUTE
- reduces the boundary-clarification action from a multi-turn exchange to one structured turn.

### Tool: 「嫌なこと + 代わりにできること」

Function:
- when a boundary is STATED, generates one replacement-plan slot;
- player must still choose or write the substitute;
- does not guarantee NPC acceptance.

### Tool: 「今の問題と、本人の悩みは同じ？」

Function:
- splits the case board into:
  - CURRENT TASK
  - PERSONAL PATTERN
- allows the player to defer the personal track without losing CLARITY on the immediate case.

Tools therefore alter play efficiency or information structure, not morality.

---

## 8. Tool acquisition revised

Tools are not exclusive to the facilitative route.

Acquisition is based on the underlying move demonstrated.

Examples:

- A direct player who asks:
  > 「どこからがダメで、どこまでならいい？」
  may earn 「どこまでなら大丈夫？」

- An analytical player who distinguishes story function from personal source material may earn:
  「嫌なこと + 代わりにできること」

- A managerial player who explicitly parks Mika's broader issue until rehearsal is stable may earn:
  「今の問題と、本人の悩みは同じ？」

- A facilitative player may earn 「友達ならどう扱う？」 through perspective reversal.

Different routes can produce different tool builds.
