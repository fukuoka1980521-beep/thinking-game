# NEW LIFE V2 OPENING — OWNER FEEDBACK: DOMAIN OVERLAP

Date: 2026-09-24
Status: REJECTED BEFORE PLAYTEST

Owner feedback:
> 「便利屋と被る」

Interpretation:
The synthetic staffing / installation-crew case overlaps too closely with the Owner's real business domain. That creates two design risks:

1. **Contamination of the playtest**
   - the Owner already knows the operational domain too well;
   - they may solve the business problem from expertise instead of engaging with the intended thinking mechanics.

2. **Product identity drift**
   - NEW LIFE should not feel like a simulation of the Owner's current handyman / staffing operations;
   - the first vertical slice should prove the mechanics in a domain independent from the Owner's own work.

Decision:
- Reject V2 before continuing the scene.
- Do not implement it.
- Keep the useful opening-gate rule (clear role / immediate problem / consequence / available actions).
- Move V3 to a neutral, non-business, non-handyman domain.

V3 domain chosen for human playtest:
**community exhibition / creative collaboration**

Reason:
- no overlap with the Owner's current work;
- still supports promises, autonomy, interpersonal interpretation, incomplete information, and consequential decisions;
- the surface problem can be made obvious without operational jargon;
- the deeper dilemma can emerge from character relationships rather than from trade expertise.
