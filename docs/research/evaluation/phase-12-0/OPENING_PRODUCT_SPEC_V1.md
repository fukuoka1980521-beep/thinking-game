## What the opening must NOT do (directive Section 4, restated as hard constraints)

- Must NOT explain the anomaly (the café anomaly / strange light / anything from the ~20%
  unexplained-or-dreamlike register) — Challenge Town canon keeps this peripheral and never
  confirmed within Chapter 1; the opening is Day 1 hour zero, before any of it has even been
  encountered.
- Must NOT state a second supernatural objective. The protagonist's only stated goal is the
  ordinary one (decide the next job/life direction).
- Must NOT tell the player what emotional conclusion to reach. Per the Story Bible's own binding
  discipline ("no scene description states what the player wants, feels, or wonders as fact"),
  the opening presents SITUATION → INFORMATION only, never a prescribed feeling.
- Must NOT over-explain. Section 3 of the PHASE 11.14 directive's lesson (avoid over-narration)
  applies here too — this is a short establishing beat, not a lore dump.

## Recent-loss detail: included or deferred?

Challenge Town canon states the protagonist recently lost his life partner (妻, unnamed), and that
this "must surface peripherally... never as an on-screen grief monologue or exposition dump" and
"recurs quietly across all 3 days." **Recommendation: deferred past the opening itself.** The
opening's job (per directive Section 4) is WHO/WHY/WHERE/DEADLINE only; the loss is a slow-reveal
texture detail (an object, a pause) that belongs to later moments (e.g., unpacking at the trial
house), not the very first establishing beat, and surfacing it in the opening would risk exactly
the "second objective" / "prescribed emotional conclusion" the directive forbids.

## Proposed opening structure (minimal, non-final wording — Product-content authoring is a
## PHASE 12.1+ task, not this design phase's)

1. **Arrival beat** — protagonist arrives at the temporary trial house in Challenge Town. States,
   through ordinary situational detail (not narrated exposition): this is temporary (a housing
   term/date, or a simple posted notice), and this is a deliberate choice he made, not something
   that happened to him.
2. **Self-statement of the goal** — a single internal or spoken line establishing the 30-day
   trial and the one-month job-decision goal, in the character's own plain voice (matches the
   Bible's personality notes: structural thinker, action-oriented, not a monologuer).
3. **First look at the town** — transition into the first MAP view (`CHALLENGE_TOWN_MAP_SYSTEM_V1.md`),
   establishing "what is this place" spatially rather than through more text.

No specific Japanese copy is authored in this phase (directive Section 26: design only, no Product
content expansion before Strategist audit).
