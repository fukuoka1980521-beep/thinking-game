## Why "NOT NEEDED IN SLICE" is not "non-canonical" (directive's explicit warning, honored)

Chinatsu/Daichi/Yui are not retracted by any document — they simply don't appear in the later,
richer `docs/world/` cast-thread material, which focuses on the six names above plus provisional
Minoru. Per the corrected authority rule, that is exactly the kind of omission that must NOT be
read as rejection. They remain available, un-superseded, canonical background/potential-future
characters; PHASE 12.1 simply has no product-design reason to activate them in a 3-NPC first slice
(same category of decision as PHASE 12.0's own choice not to include Iwata/Saeki in the first
slice — a scope decision, not a canon judgment).
