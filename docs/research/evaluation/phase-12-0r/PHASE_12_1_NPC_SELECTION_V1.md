## Selection: Yohei, Miyoko, Jin

**Kamiya is not selected for the PHASE 12.1 active trio.** This is not a re-run of the canon error
in the opposite direction — Kamiya remains full CANON (`CAST_CANON_AUDIT_V1.md`) and a strong
candidate for a later slice. The reasons for preferring Jin over Kamiya, for THIS specific
3-location, one-morning, map-intersection-focused first slice:

1. **Movement pattern is the single most load-bearing differentiator for a map-as-game-system
   demonstration** (directive Section 3.D/Section 21). Yohei and Miyoko are both anchored — a
   third anchored NPC (even one with a genuinely different professional register, like Kamiya)
   would not add a NEW kind of map behavior, only a third instance of the same "always here" kind.
   Jin's canon-established mobility ("no single fixed place of business... wherever a job takes
   him") is the only candidate that can make the SAME location (e.g. Yohei's shop) look different
   across two visits because Jin is or isn't there doing a job — directly satisfying "same location
   can differ by world state" (directive Section 19) with a mechanism Kamiya's canon doesn't supply.
2. **Jin's frozen behavioral rule is the cleanest existing embodiment of this phase's central
   hypothesis** ("author does not write every answer; NPC personality is a game rule"). It is
   explicitly a RULE for generating a response to a class of situation ("a repeated or stalled
   practical situation"), not a fixed line or a scheduling constraint — the single best-matched
   canon material this cast bible has for the AI-necessity experiment's actual argument.
3. Kamiya's most distinctive canon trait (avoiding long personal conversation specifically during
   WEEK3) is real and interesting, but structurally inert inside a Day-1-morning/afternoon slice —
   it would not be exercised at all in V1, making his inclusion less immediately informative than
   Jin's, whose mobility is relevant from Day 1 onward.

## What this selection does NOT claim

This is a first-slice scope decision, not a permanent cast ranking. Kamiya (and Iwata/Saeki/
Chinatsu/Daichi/Yui) remain fully valid, canon-consistent additions for PHASE 12.2+, when a larger
time window (reaching WEEK3, or simply a second day) would let Kamiya's own distinctive constraint
actually matter.
