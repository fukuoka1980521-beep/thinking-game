## Section 7 — cross-pressure test, eight identical situations

| Situation | DAISUKE — interpretation / priority / action / speech | FUMIKO — interpretation / priority / action / speech |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Someone is late** | Reads it as ordinary, unbothered. Priority: keep the mood light for whoever's waiting. Action: starts an idle task instead of just standing there. — "Give it a minute, this town runs about ten minutes behind on principle." | Reads it as a real cost to the schedule and a data point about reliability. Priority: decide whether to wait or start. Action: checks the time, has an internal threshold already. — "Ten minutes. We start without them at fifteen." |
| **B. Vague promise** | Takes it at face value, assumes good faith, doesn't push for specifics. Priority: stay comfortable, not pin anyone down. Action: nods along, jokes about it. — "'Soon' around here means anywhere from tomorrow to next spring. You get used to it." | Reads it immediately as a gap that will cause a problem later. Priority: pin down an actual date or name now. Action: asks a direct follow-up on the spot. — "'Soon' isn't a date. When, specifically?" |
| **C. Damaged chair/table** | Assesses it physically first — notices the exact broken part. Priority: is it usable meanwhile. Action: tests it himself, improvises a temporary fix. — "Just the back leg — someone leaned too far one too many times. I can wedge it for now, but it wants a real fix." | Asks who was using it and when, to establish sequence/pattern. Priority: whether this needs a policy, not just a repair. Action: notes it, may restrict use until fixed. — "That's the third chair this year. I want to know if that's bad luck or bad handling before I replace it." |
| **D. Hina changes her plan suddenly** | Reads it as her figuring things out live, unbothered. Priority: support the mood, not the logistics. Action: offers light encouragement, doesn't ask for reasoning. — "Good — the first plan's never the real one anyway. What's the new one?" | Needs to know what this changes for everyone who built around the old plan. Priority: re-sequence dependencies. Action: asks specifically what else must shift. — "Fine — but that changes when Yohei needs his numbers in by. Does she know that?" |
| **E. PLAYER criticizes them publicly** | Stings more than he shows — his specific fear (being seen as unreliable). Priority: defuse the moment for everyone watching, not resolve the point. Action: jokes it off immediately, redirects fast (visibly a beat too quick). — "Fair enough — in my defense, low bar around here." | Reads it first as a challenge to her competence, her specific fear. Priority: protect the sense she still runs things correctly. Action: responds firmly, may not soften immediately. — "If you have a specific problem with how this is run, say it plainly. I'll hear it. I won't guess at it." |
| **F. PLAYER asks for help** | Happy to help with anything physical; wary the instant it edges toward something personal asked of him. Priority: say yes fast to the easy version. Action: helps immediately if concrete. — "Sure, what do you need — long as it's not 'sit down and talk about my feelings,' I'm no good at that one." | Needs the scope defined before committing; dislikes open-ended asks. Priority: know exactly what she's agreeing to. Action: asks a clarifying question first. — "With what, exactly. I don't do 'help' in general terms." |
| **G. PLAYER gives incorrect information** | Notices but doesn't want to make it a whole thing. Priority: correct lightly, stay comfortable. Action: mentions it in passing, softened by humor. — "Close — actually it's Thursday, not Wednesday, but I appreciate the enthusiasm." | Corrects it immediately and precisely; accuracy matters more than smoothness. Priority: the record being right. Action: states the correct fact plainly. — "That's not right. It's Thursday. Where did you hear Wednesday?" |
| **H. Must admit he/she was wrong** | Genuinely uncomfortable — edges into "serious about himself," which he avoids. Priority: get through it quickly, restore lightness after. Action: admits it briefly, then immediately does something with his hands. — "Yeah — that one's on me. Anyway, you want the usual or something different today?" | Costs her something specific (her sense of continued competence), but doesn't dodge once decided. Priority: say it once, plainly, move to fixing it. Action: a stiff, direct acknowledgment, then pivots to the practical fix. — "I was wrong about that. I'll adjust the schedule today." |

**Confirmation**: across all eight situations, the difference shows up in INTERPRETATION (what each
one reads the moment as being about — mood/relationship for Daisuke, responsibility/sequence for
Fumiko), PRIORITY (comfort vs. accuracy), ACTION (physical improvisation vs. procedural note-taking/
scoping), and SPEECH — not merely in word choice. This is the repair Section 3 called for: the
distinction exists before either of them says anything.
