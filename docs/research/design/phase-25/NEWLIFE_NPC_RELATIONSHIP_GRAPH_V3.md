# NEW LIFE — NPC-NPC Relationship Graph V3 (PHASE_25, Section 5)

Directional, asymmetric. Values are qualitative (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH per dimension), never shown to the
player as numbers. Every one of the six has: at least one person they like (unambiguously warm),
one they misunderstand or disagree with, and one they behave differently around. Two deliberate
**mirrored-but-asymmetric misunderstandings** are built in (Yohei<->Hina; Jin<->Fumiko) — same
*shape* of misreading, different content, since both pairs involve an older, established-in-town
character whose directness reads as coldness to someone reading it from outside that norm (per
pattern P17, `PHASE_25_EXTERNAL_SOURCE_LEDGER.md` G2/G4).

---

## HINA →

- **→ Daisuke**: LIKES (warmth: HIGH, trust: MEDIUM, ease: HIGH). New-business-era camaraderie; he's
  the easiest person in town to talk to, and she knows it.
- **→ Yohei**: MISREADS (warmth: LOW-MEDIUM but improvable, trust: LOW-MEDIUM, misreading: HIGH).
  Reads his default gruffness as personal dislike specifically of her, not his baseline manner with
  every newcomer. Behaves stiff and formal around him — her most guarded register in town.
- **→ Jin**: comfortable, low-key trust (ease: HIGH, disclosure: LOW). He asks nothing of her
  emotionally, which makes him the person she's least performative around, even though she tells him
  the least.
- **→ Miyoko**: LIKES warmly (warmth: HIGH), but performs "having it together" around her more than
  around anyone else — Miyoko's uniform kindness makes Hina want to look fine, not lean on it.
- **→ Fumiko**: a little intimidated, formal (ease: LOW-MEDIUM). Fumiko's brisk directness reads as
  judgment to Hina's sensitivity, though Fumiko doesn't intend it that way.

## DAISUKE →

- **→ Yohei**: LIKES, deepest bond in town (trust: HIGH, ease: HIGH, disclosure: LOW). The one place
  he's fully relaxed — and still the one place he never raises his own stalled matters, because it
  would risk the ease itself.
- **→ Fumiko**: confides more than to anyone else (disclosure: MEDIUM-HIGH, one-directional — she
  gives more advice than she receives from him). His deepest *talking* relationship, ironically not
  his deepest *changing* one, since even here the two big stalled matters stay unspoken.
- **→ Hina**: LIKES, protective/older-sibling energy (warmth: HIGH), enjoys her energy and newness.
- **→ Jin**: MISUNDERSTANDS (warmth: MEDIUM, misreading: MEDIUM-HIGH) — friendly but surface-level;
  assumes Jin is simply fine because Jin never says otherwise, missing the pattern entirely (Daisuke's
  own blind spot, mirroring his own avoidance from the outside).
- **→ Miyoko**: warm customer/friend energy (ease: HIGH), no real depth either direction.

## JIN →

- **→ Miyoko**: LIKES (warmth: HIGH, ease: HIGH) — the chair-repair relationship is genuinely light;
  she's one of the only people who makes him laugh, and he's relatively more talkative around her
  than anyone else (still terse by any outside standard).
- **→ Fumiko**: MISREADS/MISUNDERSTOOD, mutually (respect: HIGH, warmth-perceived: LOW). He assumes
  her bluntness means she values his work only transactionally, not personally; she, from the other
  side, assumes he's fine with being treated as purely functional help because he never signals
  otherwise. Neither has ever tested the assumption.
- **→ Yohei**: neutral-professional (respect: MEDIUM-HIGH, warmth: LOW), no depth either direction.
- **→ Hina**: protective in a quiet, correctly-bounded way (care: MEDIUM-HIGH, presumption: LOW) —
  fixes her trial setup unprompted, doesn't presume closeness beyond that.
- **→ Daisuke**: fine, low-contact (ease: MEDIUM).

## YOHEI →

- **→ Daisuke**: LIKES, oldest and deepest friendship in town (trust: HIGH, ease: HIGH) — the one
  relationship where he's least gruff, most himself.
- **→ Hina**: MISREADS, slowly softening (wariness: MEDIUM declining over time, self-awareness of
  the effect: LOW). Doesn't realize his default manner reads as rejection to her specifically — a
  genuine blind spot, mirroring Hina's own misreading of him from the other side.
- **→ Jin**: respects competence, transactional warmth (respect: HIGH, warmth: LOW-MEDIUM), no real
  depth.
- **→ Miyoko**: friendly, practical, low-stakes (ease: MEDIUM).
- **→ Fumiko**: mutual old-town respect, slightly formal (respect: HIGH, warmth: MEDIUM).
- (Off-graph, not one of the six: his estranged son — the actual emotional center of his
  RELATEDNESS deficit; not a same-cast edge, load-bearing for his own arc only.)

## MIYOKO →

- **→ Fumiko**: LIKES, childhood friends, her deepest real peer bond (trust: HIGH, disclosure:
  MEDIUM — even here, under-discloses about her daughter until directly pushed).
- **→ Jin**: LIKES warmly, genuine ease (warmth: HIGH, ease: HIGH).
- **→ Hina**: warmly maternal (warmth: HIGH), risks over-helping — a gesture Hina could misread as
  pity rather than care, though Miyoko doesn't intend it that way.
- **→ Daisuke**: pleasant, surface (ease: MEDIUM).
- **→ Yohei**: pleasant, practical (ease: MEDIUM).
- (Off-graph: her daughter — the actual site of her CERTAINTY/UPDATE transformation test; not a
  same-cast edge.)

## FUMIKO →

- **→ Miyoko**: LIKES, oldest friend (trust: HIGH, warmth: HIGH — her warmest, least guarded
  relationship in town).
- **→ Daisuke**: the regular confiding partner, but ASYMMETRIC — she gives more advice than she
  receives (control-flavored even in her closest give-and-take relationship, consistent with her own
  CONTROL defense).
- **→ Jin**: MISREADS/MISUNDERSTOOD, mutually (see Jin's entry above — the same misunderstanding,
  described from her side: she assumes his quiet reliability means the relationship is purely
  functional, not personal, and has never tested that assumption either).
- **→ Yohei**: old-town respect (respect: HIGH, warmth: MEDIUM).
- **→ Hina**: behaves more clipped/formal than she consciously intends (warmth-intended: MEDIUM,
  warmth-perceived-by-Hina: LOW) — doesn't realize it reads as cold to a newcomer; a structural
  parallel to Yohei's own gruffness-misread-as-cold pattern, deliberately mirrored (same shape,
  different specific content), per pattern P17.

## NPC-NPC background motion (independent of PLAYER, per Section 12)

These relationships move on their own regardless of player presence, in the background, exactly per
the existing project discipline ("NPCs must have lives independent of PLAYER"): Yohei and Daisuke's
friendship continues at its own steady warmth; Miyoko and Fumiko's childhood-friend rhythm continues;
Jin does Hall/café-adjacent repairs for Fumiko and Miyoko on his own schedule; Daisuke and Fumiko's
confiding sessions happen with or without the player around; Yohei's own skepticism toward Hina
softens gradually on its own timeline, independent of player intervention (player evidence can only
accelerate or color this, never originate it, per the established PLAYER_INFLUENCE discipline).
