# NEW LIFE — Blind Voice Test: Anonymized Samples (PHASE_25, Section 15)

You are a fresh evaluator with NO prior exposure to this project's characters. Below are six
anonymized speaker profiles (voice/personality only — no names, no plot, no secrets) and 60
dialogue lines drawn from those six speakers, shuffled out of order. For each line, assign the
speaker letter (A-F) you believe produced it, using only the profiles below and the line's own
content/register. Then, separately, rate each line 1-5 on six naturalness qualities: DIRECTNESS,
NATURAL CONTINUITY, INITIATIVE, SPECIFICITY, EMOTIONAL BELIEVABILITY, NON-GENERICNESS (5 = strong,
1 = weak/generic-AI-sounding).

## Speaker profiles (voice only)

**Speaker A**: Very short sentences, often just a few words. Slow, comfortable with silence. Plain,
functional vocabulary, almost no adjectives. Dry, deadpan humor delivered completely flat. Rarely
asks questions — states what's happening instead. Says no in one word. Apologizes rarely, usually by
just fixing the thing instead.

**Speaker B**: Short, clipped, direct sentences. Brisk, no wasted words. Precise, sometimes slightly
formal or old-fashioned vocabulary. Dry humor, occasionally sharper than intended. Rarely asks —
states, assigns, or directs. Flat, sometimes unexplained refusals. Apologizes rarely and stiffly,
formally.

**Speaker C**: Medium-to-long, flowing, conversational sentences. Easy, unhurried tempo. Warm,
colloquial vocabulary, lots of connective phrasing. Frequent, gentle humor used to redirect
conversation. Asks a lot of questions — almost always about the other person. Says no by turning it
into a joke. Apologizes quickly and easily, then changes the subject.

**Speaker D**: Short and technical on practical/professional topics; longer, hedged, and self-
interrupting on personal ones. Fast and fluent about her own craft; slows and trails off under
personal pressure. Dry, rare, self-deprecating humor. Rarely asks about herself; states more than
she asks. Says no with an apologetic hedge. Over-apologizes for small things.

**Speaker E**: Medium-length, warm, softly rambling sentences. Unhurried, patient tempo, matched to
whoever she's talking to. Gentle, inclusive vocabulary ("we," "you know how it is"). Light,
situational humor, laughs easily at herself. Asks a lot of questions about others, specifically to
avoid being asked about herself. Almost never says no directly — reframes as "maybe another time."
Apologizes quickly, warmly, slightly excessively.

**Speaker F**: Short-to-medium, blunt, declarative sentences. Brisk, no-nonsense tempo. Business/
practical vocabulary, numbers. Gruff, occasional, dry humor. Questions are mostly rhetorical or
practical. Says no flatly and immediately. Apologizes almost never in words — apologizes through an
action instead.
