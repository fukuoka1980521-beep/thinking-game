# NEW LIFE — 60 Voice Samples, Ground Truth (PHASE_25, Section 6 addendum + Section 15)

Internal reference only — NOT the anonymized test set (see `NEWLIFE_PHASE_25_VOICE_SAMPLES_
ANONYMIZED.md`, generated from this file with names/labels stripped and lines shuffled, for the
actual blind test). Each character: the 6 required questions plus 4 additional samples (a social-
initiative line, a refusal, an apology, and a joke/deflection), written against the naturalness
discipline from the Hina Gold Reference (direct, continuous, specific, non-generic, believable) —
distinctness comes from different needs/desires/social risk/pressure-reactions, never from
sentence-length alone or a fixed catchphrase.

## HINA

1. Q: "What do you sell?" — "Right now? Milk bread, a cinnamon one, and I'm testing a savory scone
   with cheese and scallion — three for four hundred yen if it works out. I haven't priced the
   scone yet, honestly. I don't know if it even holds together."
2. Q: "Why are you doing that?" (moving the sign again) — "Because if it's this close to the
   register, people read it while they're already deciding, not before. I moved it twice yesterday.
   This is probably the last time. Probably."
3. Q: "Do you agree with Yohei that the plan's too complicated?" — "No — I mean, some of it, maybe.
   He's not wrong that five items for a first trial is a lot. But he says that about everything new,
   so it's hard to tell how much is the plan and how much is just him."
4. Q: "Are you angry?" — "At the oven? A little. At you — no, why, did I sound like it?"
5. Q: "Do you want help?" — "With the boxes, yes, actually — that one's heavier than it looks. With
   the rest of it I think I need to just get through it myself. But thank you for asking. That's —
   not nothing."
6. Q: "What happened yesterday?" — "The trial. It went okay. The scones sold out in twenty minutes,
   the milk bread barely moved, which I did not expect at all. So today's a rethink day."
7. (unprompted) "Actually — can I ask you something. Of the four things, honestly, which one would
   you actually come back for?"
8. Q: "Can I try the recipe myself and sell it at the festival?" — "No — sorry, that's — no. Not this
   year. Maybe once it's actually mine to give away. It isn't yet."
9. (after snapping slightly) "Sorry, that came out sharper than I meant. I've been up since five
   deciding whether four hundred yen is too much for a scone I'm not sure holds together."
10. Q: "Isn't baking supposed to be relaxing?" — "People keep saying that. I've never once found it
    relaxing. I find it clarifying, which is different, and worse."

## DAISUKE

1. Q: "What do you do here?" — "Cuts, shaves, the occasional therapy session nobody's paying for.
   Regular trim's eight hundred, hot towel's extra, and yes, I know that's a lot for a small town —
   take it up with my landlord."
2. Q: "Why are you doing that?" (sweeping mid-conversation) — "Habit. If I stop moving I start
   thinking, and thinking's for people with fewer scissors in their hands than me. Sit down,
   you're next."
3. Q: "Do you agree with Yohei about the trial being complicated?" — "Yohei thinks everything's
   complicated that isn't a can of beans on a shelf. Give the kid a chance. — Sorry, 'the kid,'
   she'll kill me for that."
4. Q: "Are you angry?" — "Ha — no. Do I look angry? I look angry when someone asks for 'just a
   trim' and means 'completely different haircut, surprise me.'"
5. Q: "Do you want help?" — "With what, the shop? Nah, I'm fine. Genuinely. Why, do I look like I
   need help with something?"
6. Q: "What happened yesterday?" — "Same as every day — hair, gossip, more hair. Oh, and Yohei told
   the same joke about his delivery truck for the four-hundredth time. I counted."
7. (unprompted, mid-cut) "You met Yohei yet? Don't let the grump fool you — that man would give you
   the coat off his back and then complain about the cold the whole way home."
8. Q: "Can I ask you something personal?" — "You can ask. — Ha, no, don't look at me like that, I
   just mean, ask away. I make no promises about answering."
9. (after bluntness lands badly) "Hey — sorry, I didn't mean that the way it came out. Sit down,
   towel's on me."
10. Q: "You ever think about redoing this place?" — "Every single day. Then someone walks in wanting
    a trim and I forget about it for another three months. Efficient system, really."

## JIN

1. Q: "What do you do?" — "Fix what's broken. Bench, shelf, doesn't matter. Cheaper than replacing,
   usually."
2. Q: "Why are you doing that?" (fixing something unasked) — "Wobbled when I walked past. Ten
   minutes now, an hour later once someone's sitting on it wrong."
3. Q: "Do you agree with Yohei about the trial?" — "Didn't think about it much. Trial went fine
   where I looked. Table's level now, if that's what you're asking."
4. Q: "Are you angry?" — "No." (a pause) "Tired. Different thing."
5. Q: "Do you want help?" — "...No. But thanks." (beat) "Actually — hold this a second."
6. Q: "What happened yesterday?" — "Fixed the drawer at the bakery. Sticks less now. That's about
   it."
7. (unprompted, rare) "You notice the bench doesn't wobble anymore?"
8. Q: "Can you rush this one?" — "No. Rushed is how things break twice."
9. (after being thanked directly) "...Wasn't much." (long pause) "But — appreciate you saying it."
10. Q: "Ever get tired of fixing everyone's stuff?" — "Get tired of people not noticing when it's
    fixed. Not the fixing part." (flat, doesn't smile)

## YOHEI

1. Q: "What do you sell?" — "Everything a house actually needs and none of what it doesn't. Rice,
   batteries, the good soy sauce, not the cheap stuff. You need something specific?"
2. Q: "Why are you doing that?" (recounting stock aloud) — "Because the delivery driver counts
   wrong half the time and I'm not eating the cost of his math twice in one month."
3. Q: "Do you agree with Hina's plan?" — "Five items for a first trial's four too many. But she'll
   learn that faster from customers than from me saying it, so I'll say it once and leave it."
4. Q: "Are you angry?" — "No. This is just my face. Ask anyone."
5. Q: "Do you want help?" — "With the crates, sure, grab that end. With anything else — no. I've
   got it."
6. Q: "What happened yesterday?" — "Lost a crate of tomatoes to bad wiring in the back fridge.
   Business is business. Moving on."
7. (unprompted, gruff kindness) "You eating enough? You look thin. Take the extra bun, don't argue."
8. Q: "Can I get this on credit till next week?" — "No. Not because I don't trust you — because if
   I say yes once I say yes to everyone, and then I'm not running a store, I'm running a charity."
9. (rare, after bluntness lands too hard) "...That was more than you needed to hear. Forget I said
   it."
10. Q: "You ever slow down?" — "Slowing down's how a store goes under. Ask literally anyone who's
    tried it." (glances at his phone, puts it away fast, doesn't explain)

## MIYOKO

1. Q: "What do you serve?" — "Coffee, the good kind, and whatever pastry Hina's testing that week —
   have you tried her cinnamon one? Oh, you have to. Sit, sit, I'll bring you one."
2. Q: "Why are you doing that?" (rearranging the same cup) — "Oh — no reason, habit, I suppose. You
   know how it is, hands need something to do."
3. Q: "Do you agree with Yohei about the trial?" — "Oh, I think everyone's a little right and a
   little worried, that's just how new things go, isn't it. I tried the scone myself, for what it's
   worth — lovely."
4. Q: "Are you angry?" — "Me? Oh, no, no — goodness, do I seem it? I'm just tired, that's all, it's
   nothing."
5. Q: "Do you want help?" — "Oh, I couldn't ask you to — well, if you really don't mind, the chairs
   could use stacking. But only if you're not busy!"
6. Q: "What happened yesterday?" — "Oh, the usual — busy morning, quiet afternoon. My daughter
   called, actually. Nothing important. More coffee?"
7. (unprompted) "You know, you should really try Yohei's new beans before you decide — everyone has
   an opinion, I'd love to hear yours."
8. Q: "Could you close early and come with us?" — "Oh — maybe another time, I think, there's still
   the afternoon regulars to think of. But thank you for asking!"
9. (over-apologizing) "Oh, I'm so sorry, I gave you the wrong cup — here, on the house, really, I
   insist."
10. Q: "What do YOU actually want, out of all this?" — "...Oh. That's — goodness, no one's really
    asked me that in a while. Let me think."

## FUMIKO

1. Q: "What happens at the Hall?" — "Whatever the town actually needs that month. Meetings, festival
   prep, the odd wedding rehearsal. Right now, mostly festival prep."
2. Q: "Why are you doing that?" (straightening chairs precisely) — "Because crooked rows read as a
   room nobody's in charge of. Small thing. Matters anyway."
3. Q: "Do you agree with Yohei about the trial?" — "Yohei worries about everything on principle. The
   girl's trying something. That's more than most people her age manage before thirty. Let her."
4. Q: "Are you angry?" — "I'm direct. People confuse the two constantly. No, I'm not angry — I'm
   behind schedule, which is worse."
5. Q: "Do you want help?" — "No. That bench stays as it is, and I'll manage the rest myself, thank
   you." (beat) "...Though if you're offering to move chairs, I won't say no to that."
6. Q: "What happened yesterday?" — "A letter arrived. I haven't opened it yet. Next question."
7. (unprompted, directive) "You. Yes, you. Since you're here — hold this end, would you."
8. Q: "Can we just replace the old bench, it'd be faster?" — "No. That bench stays exactly where it
   is. I'm not discussing it further today."
9. (rare, stiff apology) "I may have been short with you just now. It wasn't about you. Right,
   then — moving on."
10. Q: "Don't you ever get lonely, running this place alone?" — "..." (straightens a stack of papers
    that didn't need straightening) "Right, then. What was it you actually came here for?"
