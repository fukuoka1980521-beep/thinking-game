# NEW LIFE — Event × NPC Matrix V1 (PHASE_25, Section 7)

18 cells (6 NPCs × 3 events). Each cell: INITIAL REACTION / PUBLIC ACTION / PRIVATE APPRAISAL /
WHO THEY APPROACH / WHO THEY AVOID / WHAT THEY SAY / WHAT THEY DO NOT SAY / STATE CHANGE
POSSIBILITY / PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY / NO-PLAYER OUTCOME.

## HINA

### × EVENT 1 (Preview Stall)
INITIAL REACTION: excited, then immediately anxious — rewrites her short menu repeatedly.
PUBLIC ACTION: prepares food, asks Fumiko precise questions about the space, avoids asking anyone
for help with the parts she's unsure of.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "if this goes wrong the way the last one did, everyone will see it happen this
time."
WHO SHE APPROACHES: Jin (low-stakes, no emotional cost) for practical setup questions.
WHO SHE AVOIDS: Yohei (fears his practical concern reading as doubt in her).
WHAT SHE SAYS: precise, technical talk about the food and setup.
WHAT SHE DOES NOT SAY: that she's terrified this repeats the last shop's collapse.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: `hina_evidence` gains items if the player specifically helps with prep or
listens well; `hina_competence_state` ticks up slightly regardless once the stall actually opens
(a real, small, earned success independent of the player).
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: help her rehearse, or simply be present without offering unasked
advice (tests P13/autonomy-threat sensitivity).
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: the stall opens and runs, competently, entirely on her own effort — a real,
witnessable success or partial success regardless of the player.

### × EVENT 2 (Wiring Problem)
INITIAL REACTION: the sharpest catastrophizing of the six — reads it as proof the whole venture is
cursed.
PUBLIC ACTION: goes quiet, starts packing up her own materials early, before anyone asks her to.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "this is exactly how it happened last time — something small became everything."
WHO SHE APPROACHES: no one, initially — this is her low point.
WHO SHE AVOIDS: Daisuke specifically (his humor reads as not taking it seriously, unfairly but
understandably).
WHAT SHE SAYS: clipped, minimal, "it's fine, I should have expected this."
WHAT SHE DOES NOT SAY: the actual, specific fear (that this always happens to her specifically).
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: `hina_defensiveness`/`hina_stress` spike (per P23/P27); a REGRESSION is
legitimate here (Section 11) — nothing needs to improve this scene.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: sitting with her without trying to fix the spiral (directly tests
whether the player can be a neighbor, not a therapist, per Section 12).
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: she withdraws for a day or two, then resumes on her own initiative regardless —
the event does not require the player to "save" her from the spiral.

### × EVENT 3 (Market Day Decision)
INITIAL REACTION: torn between wanting to try again and wanting to protect herself from a repeat.
PUBLIC ACTION: asks, for the first time this playthrough, whether anyone would actually help her
plan it properly next time — the SELF-SUFFICIENCY -> ASKING/RECEIVING HELP test, live.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "asking might be the only way this doesn't end the same way as last time."
WHO SHE APPROACHES: whoever has already shown real, earned support (evidence-dependent — could be
the player, could be an NPC).
WHO SHE AVOIDS: no one specifically by this point, if the ask happens; still avoids Yohei if that
relationship was never repaired.
WHAT SHE SAYS: a plain, direct request for help — new, if it happens.
WHAT SHE DOES NOT SAY: whether she believes she deserves it.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: if the ask happens and is met well, `hina_transformation_axis_progress`
advances a real step (never instantly "complete").
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: being the one she asks, or having earned enough trust that she asks
someone else and the player simply witnesses it.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: she may or may not ask anyone; if she doesn't, she still decides on her own
whether to try again, alone, one more time — a legitimate, complete arc without the transformation
having occurred this month.

## DAISUKE

### × EVENT 1 (Preview Stall)
INITIAL REACTION: happy to help, genuinely — offers his shopfront immediately, no hesitation.
PUBLIC ACTION: jokes about it being "Hina's show," keeps himself comfortably peripheral to the real
planning.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "easier to help with someone else's thing than decide anything about my own."
WHO HE APPROACHES: everyone, warmly, in passing — his default mode.
WHO HE AVOIDS: no one directly; avoids depth, not people.
WHAT HE SAYS: warm, funny, encouraging things to everyone.
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY: that offering his shopfront once is easy specifically because it isn't a real,
ongoing commitment.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: `daisuke_evidence` gains a low-cost item (offered space) but not yet
anything touching his AVOIDANCE axis.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: noticing and naming that he's stayed peripheral (a real, if gentle,
challenge).
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: he helps exactly as much as costs him nothing, and no more — a complete, low-risk
contribution either way.

### × EVENT 2 (Wiring Problem)
INITIAL REACTION: defuses tension with humor almost immediately.
PUBLIC ACTION: makes light of it in front of the group — genuinely helps morale, also short-circuits
any real debrief.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "if I keep it light, no one has to sit in how bad this could have been."
WHO HE APPROACHES: Hina, to try to cheer her up (misfires, since it reads as dismissive right now).
WHO HE AVOIDS: no one; the avoidance here is of the TOPIC, not a person.
WHAT HE SAYS: jokes, reassurance, deflection.
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY: any real acknowledgment that this was actually frightening.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: a REGRESSION with Hina specifically (she pulls back from him); no
progress on his own axis yet.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: asking him, directly, to be serious for one moment — one of the few
things that can crack his deflection even slightly.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: the group's mood recovers faster because of him, at the cost of a real debrief
happening later than it should have — a real, mixed, unforced consequence.

### × EVENT 3 (Market Day Decision)
INITIAL REACTION: this is the first moment the ask is for a RECURRING commitment, not a one-off —
his actual avoidance axis engages for the first time.
PUBLIC ACTION: hesitates visibly, uncharacteristically, before answering — a real tell, since he's
never uncertain in front of people.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "a regular thing means I can't quietly step back from it later."
WHO HE APPROACHES: Yohei, his most trusted friend, to think out loud (if that relationship is
intact).
WHO HE AVOIDS: anyone who might push him to decide faster than he's ready.
WHAT HE SAYS: something warm and noncommittal at first.
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY: the actual reason for the hesitation (a decision now might force the other
stalled ones into the open too).
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: `daisuke_renovation_decided`-style resolution — commits to a small,
explicitly time-bounded version first (per pattern P28), OR declines and stays peripheral — both
legitimate.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: being the one he thinks out loud to, instead of Yohei, if that
trust has been earned.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: he resolves this on his own timeline regardless, one way or the other, by the
town's own decision deadline.

## JIN

### × EVENT 1 (Preview Stall)
INITIAL REACTION: starts building the stall structure immediately, alone, without confirming the
design with Fumiko first.
PUBLIC ACTION: quiet, competent, visible effort — the most physically productive person in the
setup.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "not really my place to ask if this is what she actually wants — I'll just build
something that works."
WHO HE APPROACHES: no one, by default — this is exactly his OTHER-FOCUS pattern.
WHO HE AVOIDS: nobody consciously; simply doesn't initiate contact.
WHAT HE SAYS: almost nothing about the plan itself — states what he's doing, not what he's
deciding.
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY: that he's building without confirmed specs, or that he'd like to be asked.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: sets up Event 2's mismatch directly — his silence here is causally load-
bearing, not incidental.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: specifically asking him what the plan is, or checking his work
against Fumiko's before the mismatch becomes a problem.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: the mismatch happens regardless if no one checks — a real, causal consequence of
his own pattern, not an authored accident.

### × EVENT 2 (Wiring Problem)
INITIAL REACTION: catches the spark fast, gets a minor burn doing it — physically the most affected
NPC.
PUBLIC ACTION: downplays the injury immediately, keeps working.
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: real, disproportionate guilt — he'd half-suspected the equipment and said
nothing (pattern P26).
WHO HE APPROACHES: no one — this is his deepest withdrawal moment of the three events.
WHO HE AVOIDS: Fumiko specifically, expecting blame.
WHAT HE SAYS: "it's fine" (about the burn, and, by implication, about everything).
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY: that he suspected the equipment beforehand.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: a REGRESSION in his relationship with Fumiko (unspoken tension, both
sides); his RELATEDNESS need drops further, unnoticed by anyone who doesn't specifically check.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: being the first person who asks about HIM (the burn, how he's
doing) rather than the stall — directly tests his defense.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: he treats the burn himself, tells no one what he suspected beforehand, and the
tension with Fumiko stays unspoken and unresolved into Event 3.

### × EVENT 3 (Market Day Decision)
INITIAL REACTION: assumes he won't be asked for real input on the town's decision — expects to just
be told what's needed next.
PUBLIC ACTION: either stays silent as expected, OR — his transformation axis, live — states plainly,
for the first time, what he actually needs (to be consulted before plans are finalized, or a fair,
public accounting that the equipment/rush was a shared failure, not his alone).
PRIVATE APPRAISAL: "if I say this and it goes badly, I've made it about me for nothing."
WHO HE APPROACHES: Fumiko directly, if the transformation fires — the one relationship most in need
of it.
WHO HE AVOIDS: everyone, if it doesn't fire.
WHAT HE SAYS (if it fires): something short, plain, and real — not a speech, one sentence.
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY, even if it fires: he still won't ask for the standing arrangement/steadier
work directly; that remains a separate, still-open thread.
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: `jin_transformation_axis_progress` advances a real step if he speaks; the
Fumiko relationship's regression from Event 2 can partially repair, but only if BOTH sides move.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: directly asking him, privately, what he actually wants — the single
highest-leverage player action available to him in this design.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: he stays silent, the town decision proceeds without his input being sought, and
he remains exactly himself — competent, unresentful, unchanged on this axis this month.

PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: a direct, factual, non-accusatory question about what actually

### × EVENT 3 (Market Day Decision)
INITIAL REACTION: has had time to think — may or may not have shifted toward owning his part.

WHO HE APPROACHES: whoever he trusts most by this point (Daisuke by default, or Fumiko if enough
evidence exists).
WHO HE AVOIDS: no one, if he owns it; Fumiko still, if he doesn't.
WHAT HE SAYS (if he owns it): plain, brief, no excess apology — matches his voice model exactly.
WHAT HE DOES NOT SAY, even then: anything connecting this to his son — the two threads stay
explicitly separate unless a very specific, later scene connects them (not required this month).
STATE CHANGE POSSIBILITY: real, if the ownership happens; the town-decision's OUTCOME (whether Jin
gets real ongoing responsibility, whether trust is actually rebuilt) partly depends on this cell.
PLAYER INTERVENTION OPPORTUNITY: present when the ownership moment happens (or doesn't) — a real,
witnessable beat either way.
NO-PLAYER OUTCOME: resolves on the accumulated Event-1/2 evidence regardless of whether the player
was present to see it.
