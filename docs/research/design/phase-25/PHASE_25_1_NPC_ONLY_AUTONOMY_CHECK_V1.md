## Simulation 1 — Daisuke ↔ Fumiko (conflict, post-Event-2 tension)

**Context**: days after the wiring incident (Event 2), Fumiko notices the market-day chairs still
stacked in Daisuke's shopfront, past the day they were supposed to go back to the Hall.

> **Fumiko**: "Your shopfront's still got the market-day chairs stacked in the corner. That was
> supposed to go back to the Hall Tuesday."
> **Daisuke**: "They're fine where they are, Fumiko, nobody's tripping over them—"
> **Fumiko**: "That's not the point. It was assigned to happen Tuesday. If it doesn't happen when
> it's supposed to, it doesn't happen at all — that's how these things go."
> **Daisuke**: "You're not still upset about the hot-plate thing, are you—"
> **Fumiko**: "I'm not upset. I'm asking you to move the chairs. Those are different things, even if
> you'd rather they weren't."
> **Daisuke**: (a beat) "...Fine. Tomorrow morning. I'll actually do it this time, not just say I
> will."
> **Fumiko**: "Tomorrow morning's fine." (beat) "...The equipment wasn't only your doing, for what
> it's worth. I should have checked the setup myself before the day of."
> **Daisuke**: "...Didn't expect to hear that from you." (then, predictably) "Anyway — chairs,
> tomorrow. Don't send anyone to check on me, I'll actually do it."

**Checklist**: conversation progresses — YES, four full exchanges with real movement. Someone
refuses — YES, Daisuke initially deflects and refuses to engage seriously. Someone changes
position — YES, twice: Daisuke commits to an actual dated action (a small, real AVOIDANCE->DECISION
step, `ATTEMPTED`), and Fumiko voluntarily names her own share of Event 2's responsibility, unprompted
by anyone (a real crack in her CONTROL defense, `ATTEMPTED` on her own axis too — not scored as
DELEGATION yet, but a precondition for it). State changes without PLAYER — YES, both `memories[]`
entries are created NPC-to-NPC. No one waits for PLAYER — confirmed; PLAYER is never referenced or
needed at any point in this exchange.

## Simulation 2 — Hina ↔ Yohei (disagreement, mid Event-1 planning)

**Context**: Yohei raises his usual practical concern about Hina's stock plan for the preview stall
— their established mutual-misreading dynamic, playing out with no one else present.

> **Yohei**: "Five things is too many for a first day. You'll run thin on all of them and sharp on
> none."
> **Hina**: "You said that about the trial too. I did fine."
> **Yohei**: "You did fine because you cut it down to three by the second hour. I'm saying start
> there."
> **Hina**: (a beat) "...You're not wrong that I ended up doing that anyway." (still a little
> guarded) "I just don't love being told before I've even tried."
> **Yohei**: "Wasn't telling you what to do. Telling you what I'd do." (beat) "Your call."
> **Hina**: (a small, real shift) "...Three, then. Same three as last time, maybe with the scone if
> it's ready."
> **Yohei**: "There you go." (turns back to his own stock without further comment)

**Checklist**: conversation progresses — YES. Someone refuses — YES, Hina initially pushes back on
the framing ("I just don't love being told"). Someone changes position — YES, Hina updates her
actual plan based on Yohei's input; **importantly, Yohei does NOT change** — his own gruffness and
his own DEFENSE->REALITY ACCEPTANCE axis are untouched by this scene (correctly asymmetric: this
scene is about the *business* misreading softening slightly on Hina's side, not about his son, and
not every NPC-NPC scene needs to move both parties). State changes without PLAYER — YES, Hina's
actual stock plan updates. No one waits for PLAYER — confirmed.

## Simulation 3 — Miyoko ↔ Jin (practical boundary)

**Context**: Miyoko, mid-repair-visit, starts piling on additional small asks beyond what she
originally requested — the moment tests whether Jin, who is relatively more relaxed around her than
anyone else in town, will ever set even a small boundary.

> **Miyoko**: "Jin — while you're here, would you mind also fixing the shelf in my back room? It's
> not urgent, I just keep forgetting to ask properly."
> **Jin**: "Which shelf." (already checking)
> **Miyoko**: "Oh, the one behind the counter — actually, and the cupboard door too, if you have
> time, and maybe—"
> **Jin**: (cuts in, not rude, just plain) "One thing today. Shelf's fine. Cupboard's a different
> day."
> **Miyoko**: (a little surprised, then warm) "...Oh! Of course, sorry, I do that, I just pile things
> on once I've got you here."
> **Jin**: (softens, since it's her specifically) "Not a complaint." (beat) "Just — one thing goes
> right if it's one thing."
> **Miyoko**: "You don't usually say that much at once."
> **Jin**: (a rare, small joke) "Don't usually get asked for three things at once, either."

**Checklist**: conversation progresses — YES. Someone refuses — YES, Jin declines the expanded scope
plainly, without conflict. Someone changes position — YES, Jin, uncharacteristically, states a real
boundary and even offers a small piece of reasoning for it unprompted (a genuine, if tiny,
OTHER-FOCUS->OWN VOICE step, `ATTEMPTED`, specifically because it's Miyoko — consistent with the
relationship graph's note that he's relatively more talkative around her than anyone else). Miyoko
accepts without resistance (consistent with her own LOW-Agency, accommodating profile — she adapts
rather than pushing). State changes without PLAYER — YES, a real evidence-adjacent beat for Jin's
own axis is created NPC-to-NPC. No one waits for PLAYER — confirmed.
