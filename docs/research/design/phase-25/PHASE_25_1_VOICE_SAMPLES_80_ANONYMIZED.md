# NEW LIFE — Second Blind Voice Test: 80 Anonymized Samples (PHASE_25.1, Section 8)

You are a fresh evaluator with NO prior exposure to this project's characters (even if you evaluated
a similar test before, treat this as entirely new — these are new lines and new speaker letters).
Below are six anonymized speaker profiles (voice/personality only) and 80 dialogue lines, shuffled.
For each line, assign the speaker letter (P/Q/R/S/T/U) you believe produced it, using only the
profiles and each line's own content/register. Then rate each line's overall naturalness 1-5
(DIRECTNESS, NATURAL CONTINUITY, INITIATIVE, SPECIFICITY, EMOTIONAL BELIEVABILITY, NON-GENERICNESS
combined; 5 = strong human-sounding dialogue, 1 = generic/robotic), flagging any line that reads as
generic or "AI customer-service" rather than a specific person. Finally, self-assess which pairs
were hardest to discriminate and why, and specifically whether any two speakers among P/Q/R/S/T/U
felt like the same person more than once.

## Speaker profiles (voice only)

**Speaker P**: Warm, softly rambling sentences, unhurried. Frequent, gentle questions aimed at the
other person, specifically to avoid being asked about herself. Light, self-deprecating humor. Almost
never refuses directly — reframes as "maybe another time." Apologizes quickly, warmly, a little
excessively.

**Speaker Q**: Very short sentences, comfortable with long silence. Plain, almost adjective-free
vocabulary. States what's happening rather than asking. Refuses in one word or a short flat phrase.
Rarely apologizes in words — shows care or regret through a small action or a long pause instead.
Notices and comments on physical repair/condition unprompted.

**Speaker R**: Notices the physical, practical condition of things and people first, and comments on
THAT rather than on feelings. Warm and genuinely curious about other people specifically; deflects
any question about himself with humor aimed back at the asker or at a third person. Talks about
work/tools/timing as a substitute for talking about emotions. Occasionally, briefly, admits to
postponing something of his own, lightly, self-aware, never dwelling on it.

**Speaker S**: Short, blunt, declarative sentences. Business/practical vocabulary, numbers. Refuses
flatly and immediately. Apologizes almost never in words — apologizes through an action instead. Any
rare moment of vulnerability is shut down and deflected within the same breath.

**Speaker T**: Short, precise, sometimes formal sentences. Frames things through responsibility,
sequence, dates, and consequence to a place/institution rather than to herself personally. Treats not
knowing something as a plain, procedural next-step ("I'll know by X") rather than something to hide
or soften. Corrects imprecise information or vague commitments immediately and plainly. Stiff, brief,
formal apologies. Assigns tasks directively, on the spot.

**Speaker U**: Fast, fluent, technical, and specific about her own craft/work (numbers, ingredients,
methods); short and hedged, self-interrupting, trailing off under personal pressure. Dry, rare,
self-deprecating humor. Over-apologizes for small things. Holds her own opinion and can disagree
gently without conceding easily.
