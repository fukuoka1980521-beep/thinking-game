### OWNER_REAL_CORE patterns (reused, already-verified — see provenance doc for full citation)

**P01** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-01)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: criticism of something I made is criticism of ME.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: the creator has to become the harshest critic of their own work before
anyone else's critique can land as useful rather than as an attack.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: confident and fluent discussing the work's specifics; brittle the instant the
work's *value* is questioned.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: rationalization — explains why the criticism doesn't apply, rather than
sitting with it.
TRIGGER: a real user/observer states a plain, specific problem with the work.
TURNING_POINT: the same problem recurs from an independent second source, or the person notices it
themselves before being told.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a real customer/participant reaction to something the character made or
organized.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: do not reproduce the Owner's specific product/business details — use only the
defensiveness-then-ownership shape.

**P02** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-02)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: I'll get to the real decision later; right now I just need to keep moving.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: repeatedly skipping the upfront, uncomfortable groundwork produces far more
rework than the groundwork would have cost.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: capable and fast once a direction is picked; avoids being the one who picks it.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: busyness — stays occupied with adjacent tasks that feel like progress.
TRIGGER: an external deadline or another person's decision forces the postponed choice into the
open.
TURNING_POINT: the cost of not deciding becomes larger and more visible than the cost of deciding.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a stalled personal/business decision that a shared town event forces to a
head.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: keep the avoidance abstract (a decision, not a specific real project).

**P03** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-03)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: cutting this one corner under pressure is harmless — it's a small thing.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: the corner cut was load-bearing; the shortcut quietly damages the very
thing it was meant to protect.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: prides himself on practical, businesslike judgment; the one time judgment
actually fails is exactly a "practical" call made under real pressure.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: control — decides alone, fast, to avoid the discomfort of asking or waiting.
TRIGGER: real resource/budget pressure with a visible deadline.
TURNING_POINT: the consequence becomes visible to someone the character respects.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a stock/resource decision made under festival-prep time pressure.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: no real financial figures or the Owner's specific asset/technical context.

**P04** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-04)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: I already know what's wrong here — I've seen this exact problem before.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: the assumption, however experienced, is wrong until checked against what's
actually, physically, currently true.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: highly competent from real repeated experience; that same experience is what
makes him skip verification.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: none needed as a flaw exactly — closer to an efficient habit that occasionally
misfires; when it does, mild embarrassment, quickly corrected.
TRIGGER: a task that superficially resembles a familiar one but differs in one physical detail.
TURNING_POINT: re-examining the actual object/situation up close, not from memory.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a repair/practical task assumed routine that turns out to need real,
close inspection.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: keep it to a generic craft/repair skill, not the Owner's specific technical
domain.

**P05** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-05)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: the checklist is complete, therefore the job is done.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: technical/procedural completion and real human success are different
facts; something human can still be missing even when every box is checked.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: highly organized and thorough; the thoroughness itself becomes a place to hide
from a less measurable, more human gap.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: over-preparation as a substitute for a harder, more personal action.
TRIGGER: noticing the checklist is done but something still feels short.
TURNING_POINT: choosing to do the uncomfortable, unlisted human thing (a personal invitation, a
direct ask) instead of trusting the list alone.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: event/prep organizing where attendance/participation depends on more than
logistics.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: no specific real checklist content from the Owner's own work.

**P06** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-06)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: things run fine without me stepping in — I don't need to intervene.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: independence/autonomy genuinely helps, right up to a point where a
specific, well-timed human intervention is still what actually moves things forward; autonomy alone
plateaus.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: values letting things run themselves; secretly needs occasional recognition that
someone still checks in.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: withdrawal disguised as respecting others' independence.
TRIGGER: a long stretch of no contact/no check-in, without either side clearly initiating.
TURNING_POINT: one specific, well-timed gesture from someone else breaks the stall — not a long
process, a single well-placed moment.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a standing offer or relationship that stays stalled until someone
specifically, deliberately re-opens it.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: keep this abstract — a general "stalled arrangement," not the Owner's own
AI-autonomy work context.

**P07** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-07)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: I already know what benefit this will deliver — no need to check.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: replacing the assumed benefit with a real, measured observation changes
the actual decision, sometimes substantially.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: confident and persuasive when presenting assumptions as if verified; the
persuasiveness is exactly what delays anyone (including herself) actually checking.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: rationalization dressed as confidence.
TRIGGER: a real, small, cheap opportunity to actually measure/observe instead of assume.
TURNING_POINT: the real data contradicts the assumption in a way too specific to explain away.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a decision based on assumed customer/family preference that a real
conversation or trial run overturns.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: no real business proposal specifics from the Owner's own consulting work.

**P08** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-08)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: if I just explain clearly enough, the other person will do the sensible
thing.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: people act far more strongly and durably on a conclusion they feel they
reached themselves than on one delivered to them, however correct.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: genuinely wants to help; the "helping" instinct (explaining, advising) is
exactly what can crowd out the other person's own process of arriving there.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: over-helping as a substitute for waiting/trusting.
TRIGGER: someone visibly struggling with a decision the character has an opinion about.
TURNING_POINT: choosing to listen without pushing a side, and the other person reaching their own
conclusion anyway — sometimes the same one, sometimes not.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a listener character genuinely withholding a "correct" answer during
someone else's real dilemma.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: no specific real sales scenario from the Owner's career.

**P09** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-09)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: enough pleasant conversation, on its own, adds up to something meaningful.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: without real incidents, consequences, and things people actually remember
and reference later, pleasant conversation alone stays weightless.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: articulate and easy to talk to; the ease itself can mask that nothing is
actually changing or being risked.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: pleasantness as a substitute for consequence.
TRIGGER: a moment where a real stake, promise, or consequence is actually on the table.
TURNING_POINT: the character does something with a real, remembered cost or benefit, not just talk.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a relationship that only progresses once a concrete, remembered, callback-
worthy incident occurs between two people — never from small talk volume alone.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: this is a project-level design thesis as much as a personal pattern; use it
to police EVERY character's transformation logic, not just assign it to one NPC.

**P10** — SOURCE_TYPE: OWNER_REAL_CORE (OWNER-10)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: a real failure is simply a loss.
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: a disclosed, well-understood failure is frequently the direct trigger for
the next, better method — the failure itself does useful work if looked at honestly.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: values getting things right the first time; the actual pattern of real growth
in this person's life keeps arriving through failure, not foresight.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: mild self-criticism that can tip into rumination if not caught.
TRIGGER: a plan visibly not working, publicly or semi-publicly.
TURNING_POINT: treating the failure as information to redesign from, and actually doing so, rather
than repeating the same approach.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: an initial joint-project approach that visibly fails, followed by a real,
better second attempt.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: use as a structural principle for Event 2 -> Event 3's shape, not necessarily
one character's personal arc.

### EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (grounded in the four cited frameworks)

**P11** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G2, Interpersonal Circumplex)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "if I disagree with someone directly, it will damage the relationship."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: a LOW-Agency/HIGH-Communion person can disagree and still be experienced as
warm — agreement and warmth are separate axes, not the same thing.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: wants closeness; equates closeness with never causing friction.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: silent compliance, later resentment.
TRIGGER: a real, low-stakes disagreement where honesty is actually safe to test.
TURNING_POINT: disagreeing once, safely, and the relationship not breaking.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a character who has never told a close NPC they were wrong about something
small, and finally does.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: keep general; not tied to any one real person.

**P12** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G2, Interpersonal Circumplex)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "asking for help means I'm not competent."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: a HIGH-Agency, LOW-Communion-expression person often reads help-seeking as
a status loss, when in most communities it reads as basic trust, not weakness.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: freely gives help to others (which feels like agency exercised well); cannot
receive it (which would require lowering perceived agency).
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: over-functioning — doing more than necessary alone rather than delegating or
asking.
TRIGGER: a task genuinely too large for one person.
TURNING_POINT: accepting help once and the world not ending; possibly the help being offered by
someone whose own competence the character already respects, lowering the perceived cost.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a capable, self-sufficient character finally accepting real help on a
shared project.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: general shape only.

**P13** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G3, Self-Determination Theory)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I'm being helpful."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: from the receiving side, unsolicited over-help can read as a threat to
autonomy — "you don't think I can do this myself" — regardless of the helper's genuine intent.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: high-relatedness motivation (wants to be close, useful); the method chosen
(control-flavored helping) undermines the other person's autonomy need.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: over-helping.
TRIGGER: someone the character cares about visibly struggling.
TURNING_POINT: being asked, directly, to back off — and actually doing it, converting help into
availability instead of intervention.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a caretaking character whose help is gently declined or pushed back on by
someone they're trying to support.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: general shape only.

**P14** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G3, Self-Determination Theory)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I don't need anyone; I'm fine on my own."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: relatedness is a need, not a preference — chronic self-sufficiency that
suppresses it produces a slow, often unacknowledged cost (irritability, flatness, a private sense of
being unseen) even when the person insists they're fine.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: outwardly self-sufficient and competent; privately under-resourced on
connection, without framing it that way even to themselves.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: withdrawal, busyness, deflecting any question about how they're actually
doing.
TRIGGER: an unprompted, specific gesture of care from someone else, not owed or transactional.
TURNING_POINT: letting that gesture land instead of deflecting it — visibly, if briefly.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a quietly under-thanked character receiving one specific, sincere,
unprompted acknowledgment.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: general shape only.

**P15** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G3, Self-Determination Theory)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I have to keep proving I'm still good at this / still needed."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: competence satisfaction that depends entirely on one narrowing domain
(e.g., a former professional role) becomes fragile exactly when that domain shrinks (retirement,
a changed life stage) — the need doesn't go away, only its old supply does.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: outwardly capable and in-charge; privately measuring worth against a shrinking
yardstick.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: control — over-managing a smaller domain (a hobby, a small institution, an
object) to keep some visible proof of continued competence.
TRIGGER: an offer of help or change to that smaller domain, read as a threat to the last visible
proof of relevance.
TURNING_POINT: finding a genuinely new, smaller-scale but real source of competence, rather than
defending the old one at all costs.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a retired or aging character refusing help/delegation on a small domain they
still control.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: general life-stage shape only.

**P16** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G4, CAPS / Person×Situation)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "this person is just difficult" (a trait judgment).
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: the same person can be warm in one specific situation-type and cold in
another — the pattern is an if-then signature, not a fixed trait; a single incident is a bad sample
of the whole signature.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: appears inconsistent to an outside observer; is actually behaving very
consistently according to their own situational rule ("warm with X kind of moment, guarded with Y
kind").
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: none per se — this is a modeling pattern for the OBSERVER's mistake, not a
character flaw; used to design a character whose apparent inconsistency is legible once the
if-then rule is understood.
TRIGGER: an observer generalizing from one bad interaction to "that's just how they are."
TURNING_POINT: the observer sees the same person warm in a different, specific situation-type, and
revises the generalization.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: the player misjudging an NPC from one encounter, then witnessing a
genuinely different, equally real side of them in a different context.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: use as a structural design principle across the whole cast, not a single
NPC's biography.

**P17** — SOURCE_TYPE: EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_PATTERN (G2 + G4 combined)
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I'm always the reasonable one; other people are the problem."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: a HIGH-Agency, MEDIUM-Communion person's own bluntness/directness reads
very differently to a LOW-Agency listener than it does from the inside — the same words land as
plain fact to the speaker and as an attack to the listener; both readings are locally "reasonable"
given each person's own baseline.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: prides self on directness/honesty; genuinely doesn't register how often it
lands as harsh, because it doesn't feel harsh from the inside.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: none named as pathology — a real, ordinary interpersonal-style mismatch.
TRIGGER: direct, blunt feedback delivered plainly, received by someone conflict-averse.
TURNING_POINT: being told plainly, by someone they respect, how it actually landed — and adjusting
delivery without abandoning honesty.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: two older, established-in-town characters whose blunt/gruff styles each
read as coldness to a newer, more sensitive character, without either of them noticing the pattern
in themselves.
DO_NOT_COPY_LITERALLY: general interaction-style mismatch only.

### FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS (explicitly invented shapes, not claimed as real)

**P18** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "if I stay useful to everyone equally, no one will ever have a reason to
leave."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: uniform warmth given to everyone can make it invisible when the giver
personally needs something back — nobody reads it as a request because it never looks different
from ordinary generosity.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: appears endlessly patient and equal-handed; privately keeps score of who
notices her, and is quietly hurt when no one does.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: performed cheerfulness, over-helping, uniform treatment as camouflage.
TRIGGER: a moment requiring a personal preference or ask, not a service.
TURNING_POINT: stating a specific personal want out loud, to a specific person, instead of hoping
it's noticed.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a warm, universally-liked character who has never once said what SHE wants.

**P19** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I already know what the person I love actually wants — I don't need to
ask."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: a certainty formed years ago, never updated, may be describing an earlier
version of that person rather than who they are now.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: attentive and perceptive about everyone else's needs professionally; incurious
about this one specific person's actual, current wishes.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: rationalization ("I know them better than they know themselves").
TRIGGER: the other person's own words directly contradicting the long-held assumption.
TURNING_POINT: asking the actual, current question instead of continuing to act on the old
assumption.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a parent character certain about what their grown child wants, who has not
actually asked in a long time.

**P20** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I show I care by fixing things, not by saying things."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: unasked-for competence reads as ordinary service to most observers unless
specifically named — the intended message ("I care about you") often never actually arrives.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: genuinely warm underneath; the chosen expression (silent, skilled action) fails
to transmit the warmth to anyone who isn't already looking for it.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: substituting action for disclosure.
TRIGGER: someone specifically naming and thanking the effort, rather than just benefiting from it.
TURNING_POINT: for once, saying the plain thing instead of just doing the plain thing.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a quietly competent character whose real feelings are only ever expressed
through fixed things, never words.

**P21** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "if I let go of control of this one small thing, I'm admitting I'm no longer
needed."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: delegating a small, specific task doesn't erase relevance — it usually
frees capacity for a different, still-needed contribution.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: values the institution/place surviving past her; resists the specific,
practical steps (letting someone else help run it) that would actually make that survival more
likely.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: control, expressed as refusal to change a small physical thing (an object, a
routine) standing in for the larger, harder-to-name fear.
TRIGGER: a genuine, practical offer of help with the small thing.
TURNING_POINT: accepting the small delegation once, and the sky not falling.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: an older institution-keeper refusing a small, sensible change to something
she's guarded for years.

**P22** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "reaching out now would mean admitting I let this go on too long."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: the length of a silence is not proof it can't be broken — it's just proof
neither side has broken it yet.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: capable of warmth and generosity toward near-strangers; frozen specifically with
the one person whose response would actually matter most.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: control/gruffness as a wall against a specific vulnerability, generalized
outward to everyone so the specific target isn't obvious.
TRIGGER: real time passing without action, made visible by an outside event (a reminder, a season,
someone else's example).
TURNING_POINT: making the first small, low-stakes contact — not a full resolution, just contact.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: an estranged-family thread where the call itself, not reconciliation, is the
actual milestone.

**P23** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "getting this exactly right before anyone sees it is what makes it good."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: perfectionism that delays showing work indefinitely also delays the only
process (real feedback) that could make the work actually better.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: cares intensely about quality; the caring itself produces the exact behavior
(withholding, endless revision) most likely to prevent quality from improving.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: perfectionism as control.
TRIGGER: an external deadline that forces the work into the open before it feels ready.
TURNING_POINT: real feedback after the forced reveal turns out to be survivable and useful, not
catastrophic.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a craftsperson forced by circumstance to show unfinished or imperfect work.

**P24** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "if I make people laugh, the conversation won't go somewhere uncomfortable."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: humor deployed specifically to redirect a conversation away from the self
works nearly every time — which is exactly the problem, since it also means the self is never
actually discussed.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: genuinely funny and well-liked; the same skill that makes people feel at ease
around him is what keeps anyone from actually getting close.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: humor/deflection.
TRIGGER: someone refusing to let the joke land as a full stop, and asking the real question again.
TURNING_POINT: answering once, briefly, without the joke — even a small, incomplete answer.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a warm, funny character whose deflections have worked on literally everyone
until one person doesn't let it go.

**P25** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "a small, harmless mistake made under pressure doesn't need to be owned
publicly — it'll blow over."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: an unowned mistake in a shared project tends to metastasize into a
blame-search that damages trust more than the original mistake did.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: values being seen as competent and reliable; the instinct to protect that image
(deflect, minimize) is what actually threatens it once the deflection itself is noticed.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: minimization, quiet deflection of responsibility.
TRIGGER: a visible, semi-public failure with more than one plausible cause.
TURNING_POINT: naming one's own specific part in it, unprompted, before being cornered into it.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a joint-project failure with genuinely distributed responsibility, where
early, voluntary ownership changes the whole group's trajectory.

**P26** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "I noticed the risk earlier, but it wasn't really my place to say
anything."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: staying silent about a known risk to avoid seeming presumptuous can make
the eventual failure partly attributable to the silence itself, not just to whoever executed the
flawed plan.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: highly competent and observant; the same low-agency, other-focused style that
makes him easy to work alongside is what keeps him from raising a concern before it becomes a
problem.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: withdrawal, deference.
TRIGGER: noticing a specific, concrete risk in someone else's plan or work.
TURNING_POINT: raising the concern once, plainly, even though it isn't strictly his place.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a quiet, competent character who saw a failure coming and said nothing —
and has to decide whether to speak up the next time.

**P27** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "everyone else seems to have this figured out; I must just be behind."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: most people's outward composure is itself a performance managing the same
underlying uncertainty — the comparison is between one person's real anxiety and everyone else's
edited surface.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: outwardly capable in her actual craft; privately convinced she's one visible
mistake away from being exposed as not good enough.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: over-preparation, rumination, catastrophizing minor feedback.
TRIGGER: any public evaluation of her own work, however small.
TURNING_POINT: a real, survivable public failure that doesn't produce the catastrophe she expected.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a first real public test of new work, where the actual result is more
survivable and instructive than the anticipated one.

**P28** — SOURCE_TYPE: FICTIONAL_SYNTHESIS
INITIAL_INTERPRETATION: "committing to a recurring responsibility means I can never change my mind
again."
ACTUAL_OR_LATER_REALITY: most recurring commitments are renegotiable later — the fear is of
permanence that the commitment itself doesn't actually carry.
CORE_CONTRADICTION: reliable and consistent once inside a commitment; the act of entering one feels
disproportionately final and frightening beforehand.
DEFENSE_OR_AVOIDANCE: avoidance of the decision point itself, rather than the responsibility.
TRIGGER: being asked to commit to something ongoing, not one-off.
TURNING_POINT: committing to a small, explicitly time-bounded version first, and it going fine.
POSSIBLE_GAME_PRESSURE: a town decision asking someone to take on a recurring (not one-time) role.
